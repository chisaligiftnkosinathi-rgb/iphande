# iPhande V1 Production Boundary

## The First Production Goal
A steward can register, become visible, receive customer interest, and continue daily business.

## The Golden Rule for V1
No feature enters this application unless it explicitly helps a steward:
1. Register
2. Become visible
3. Get contacted
4. Preserve proof
5. Follow up

## The First Screen
The entry point to iPhande must be perfectly clean, devoid of developer jargon, and immediately human:

> **iPhande**
> Make your work visible.
> Build trust through real service.
>
> [ Register Business ]
> [ Sign In ]
> [ Explore Businesses ]
