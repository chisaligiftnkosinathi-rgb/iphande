# 📦 SANAS ERP Implementation & Adoption Plan (Procurement-Grade)

This document refines the project's rollout strategy from an engineering-led plan to a procurement-safe institutional adoption plan, focusing on risk containment and compliance assurance for a public institution like SANAS.

---

## 1. Core Principle: Procurement-Safe Framing

The central shift is from proposing "deployment phases" to proposing **"low-risk adoption stages with institutional safeguards"**. SANAS is not buying technology phases; they are buying risk containment over time.

The core identity of the project is now:

> "A government-safe ERP adoption pathway with embedded audit intelligence"

---

## 2. The Institutional Adoption Layer (IAL)

This is the critical layer that makes the proposal a safe procurement decision. It includes:

*   Migration safety guarantees
*   Rollback strategy
*   Dual-run period with legacy systems
*   Audit validation checkpoints per phase
*   Sign-off gates between stages

---

## 3. Controlled Adoption Strategy

### Stage 1: Validation & Parallel Run (Low Risk)

*   **Scope**: Limited SANAS departments selected for a non-production pilot.
*   **Action**: The system runs in parallel with existing processes. All data entered into the legacy system is mirrored into the new platform.
*   **Authority**: No production authority is transferred. The legacy system remains the system of record.
*   **Focus**: Validating data correctness, workflow accuracy, and the audit trail generated by the **SANAS Compliance Kernel**.

---

### Stage 2: Controlled Operational Adoption (Medium Risk)

*   **Scope**: Full rollout of Procurement and HR modules to the pilot departments.
*   **Action**: The **Axionyx ERP Core** modules are activated for daily operations.
*   **Authority**: The legacy system remains authoritative for final financial approval and historical records. The new system becomes the system of execution for operational tasks.
*   **Safeguard**: The **SANAS Kernel** logs a parallel, tamper-evident audit trail, which is reconciled against the legacy system's logs.

---

### Stage 3: Field Operations Integration (Operational Risk Layer)

*   **Scope**: Rollout of the **iPhande Mobile Operations Layer** to a select group of field inspectors.
*   **Action**: Mobile audit capture, on-site evidence upload, and task management are performed via the mobile application.
*   **Authority**: The mobile application becomes the primary tool for field data capture for the pilot group.
*   **Safeguard**: Offline data capture and synchronization are validated. All field events are verified against the central ledger in the **SANAS Kernel**.

---

### Stage 4: Full Governance Transition (High Trust Stage)

*   **Scope**: Full organizational transition to the new platform.
*   **Action**: The **SANAS Kernel** becomes the official, auditable source-of-record for all institutional actions. The **Axionyx ERP Core** becomes the primary system of execution.
*   **Authority**: Full authority is transferred to the new platform.
*   **Safeguard**: Legacy systems are transitioned to a read-only, archival role. Final data migration and reconciliation are completed and signed off.

---

## 4. Institutional Safety Mechanisms

*   **✔ Dual-System Mode**: The system runs in parallel with the legacy ERP before any authoritative takeover, allowing for safe validation.
*   **✔ Audit Reconciliation Layer**: Every action is cross-validated between the new and legacy systems during the parallel run stage.
*   **✔ Rollback Capability**: Any stage can be programmatically reversed to the prior state without data loss during the initial phases.
*   **✔ Certification Gate Model**: Each stage requires formal sign-off from SANAS management and audit teams before progression to the next stage is permitted.

---

## 5. Support & Cost Model (Procurement-Focused)

### Support Model
*   **On-site SANAS Deployment Team**: For the initial rollout and training period.
*   **National Remote Support Hub**: For ongoing operational support.
*   **Compliance Update Channel**: For regulatory-driven updates (PFMA, POPIA, etc.).
*   **Monthly Audit Assurance Reporting**: Proactive reports generated from the SANAS Kernel to demonstrate system integrity.

### Cost Model
Government procurement prioritizes predictable lifecycle cost. The model is framed as:
1.  **Implementation & Integration Cost**: A once-off fee for deployment, data migration, and training.
2.  **Annual Compliance & Support Contract**: A predictable annual fee covering support, maintenance, and mandatory compliance updates.
3.  **Optional Module Expansion Licensing**: Fees for activating additional ERP modules in the future.

---

## 6. Risk Framing

*   **Data Migration Risk**: Contained via the **Dual-System Mode** and **Audit Reconciliation Layer**.
*   **Operational Risk**: Phased by department and function, ensuring controlled adoption.
*   **Governance Risk**: Absorbed by the **SANAS Kernel's** tamper-evident audit layer, providing superior evidence to legacy systems.
*   **Adoption Risk**: Mitigated via the on-site deployment team, a "train-the-trainer" model, and the staged rollout.
