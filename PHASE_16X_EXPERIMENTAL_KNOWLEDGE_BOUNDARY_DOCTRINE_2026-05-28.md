## Experimental Knowledge Boundary Doctrine

**Date:** 2026-05-28

---

## 1. Canonical Production Doctrine

Artifacts that directly govern executable architecture and system behavior:

- API contracts
- Continuity doctrine
- Replay doctrine
- Stewardship boundaries
- UX doctrine
- Media preservation doctrine

**Rule:** These documents and schemas influence real system behavior and must be maintained as canonical, versioned, and tightly governed.

---

## 2. Experimental Reflection Archive

Artifacts for symbolic, spiritual, or national thought experiments, covenant reflections, and exploratory frameworks:

- Thought experiments
- Symbolic explorations
- National frameworks
- Covenant reflections

**Rule:** These are not executable governance. They do not automatically affect runtime behavior or constitutional doctrine unless explicitly promoted through a formal process.

---

## 3. Research Surfaces

Artifacts grounded in observed human patterns and anthropology:

- Relationship steward answers
- Humane systems shaping
- Continuity anthropology
- Trade observations

**Rule:** These are valuable for informing doctrine but are not themselves governance or executable truth.

---

## 4. Critical Boundary

> "Symbolic or spiritual exploration must never silently mutate operational governance."

**Rule:** All symbolic, spiritual, or experimental artifacts must remain structurally and procedurally separate from canonical production doctrine. Promotion or migration of content from experimental/reflection/research surfaces to canonical doctrine requires explicit review and governance action.
