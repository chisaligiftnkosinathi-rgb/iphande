# iPhande Product Flow Doctrine

## 1. What iPhande Is
- A lightweight business continuity OS for everyday African entrepreneurs.
- A governed opportunity continuity system.
- A continuity layer, not a social media replacement.

## 2. What iPhande Is Not
- Not a social network.
- Not a CRM clone.
- Not a noisy feed.
- Not an AI gimmick.
- Not a marketplace ranking platform.
- Not pay-to-play.

## 3. Core Pain Point
- Informal and small businesses lose opportunity continuity across WhatsApp, Facebook, TikTok, calls, and manual follow-ups.
- Leads are scattered.
- Follow-ups are forgotten.
- Business activity has no replay.

## 4. Core Loop
Business Identity
→ Create Business Post
→ Share to Social Media
→ Customer Requests Quote
→ Business Follows Up
→ Replay Captures Activity
→ Repeat / Grow

## 5. Screen Responsibilities
- **Home:** What should I do next?
- **Profile:** Who am I as a business?
- **Content Tool:** What should I post/share?
- **Quote Requests:** Who responded?
- **Opportunities:** What needs follow-up?
- **Replay:** What happened over time?
- **Giving:** How can I support voluntarily after value?

## 6. Product Rules
- Business identity drives everything.
- Content generator is a tool, not a destination.
- Quote requests are first-class opportunity records.
- Replay is operational memory.
- Giving is voluntary and never blocks access.
- No rankings, trust scores, hidden scoring, or fake authority.

## 7. Mobile UX Rule
Every screen must answer one clear question.
If a screen cannot answer one clear question, it is too broad.

## 8. Acceptance Criteria
- User always knows the next step.
- No duplicate business identity questions.
- No random AI behavior.
- No feature is hidden behind payment.
- Quote requests connect back to replay/opportunities.
- Social media remains the discovery layer; iPhande remains the continuity layer.
