# iPhande Content Generation Protocol

## Standards for Generated Content
- Truthful
- Warm
- Business-aware
- No false guarantees
- No manipulation
- No fear pressure
- No spam
- No fake scarcity
- No impersonation
- User must review before sharing
- WhatsApp/Facebook links are for share-preparation only, not auto-posting

---

## Content Structure
1. Opening line
2. Service explanation
3. Offer/context
4. Area/location
5. Trust statement
6. Call to action
7. Contact/profile link

---

## Review & Approval
- All generated content must be reviewed by the user before sharing.
- No content is auto-posted or sent without user action.
- Content must be respectful, relevant, and appropriate for the business line.
