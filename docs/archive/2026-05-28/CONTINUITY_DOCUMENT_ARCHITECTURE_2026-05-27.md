# CONTINUITY DOCUMENT ARCHITECTURE

**Date:** 2026-05-27

## Purpose
Define the architectural foundation for long-form knowledge and document continuity (Advanced PDF Surfaces / The Scroll System) within iPhande.

Documents (PDFs) are not random detached blobs. They are first-class continuity objects. They preserve ministry teachings, business proposals, governance doctrine, and operational archives in a structured, replay-aware, dignified way.

## Core Doctrine
> A continuity document preserves declared context. It does not manufacture authority.

## 1. ContinuityDocument Entity
The `ContinuityDocument` is a replay-aware wrapper around binary data. It elevates a raw file into a governed artifact. A document cannot exist in the system without an owning steward and a specific place within the causal river.

## 2. Document Types (Artifact Typology)
Artifacts are typed to preserve their operational meaning.
* **Ministry & Community:** `doctrine_scroll`, `ministry_teaching`, `study_guide`, `devotional`
* **Business & Commerce:** `business_proposal`, `price_list`, `catalogue`, `quotation`, `capability_statement`
* **Governance & Memory:** `operational_archive`, `case_evidence`, `steward_reflection`
* **Generic:** `pdf_document`

## 3. Visibility Rules
Visibility is a declared, governed state, not an implicit assumption.
* **Public:** Visible on the Business Home or public-facing timeline.
* **Steward-Only:** Private operational archives and internal reflections.
* **Lineage-Restricted:** Visible only to parties involved in a specific quote or evidence chain.
* *Rule:* Visibility does not equal endorsement by the system.

## 4. Lineage and Replay Relationship
Every `ContinuityDocument` must be bound to the `continuity_events` ledger.
* Ingesting a document emits: `document_ingested`
* Generating a document emits: `document_generated`
* The document record must contain a `parent_event_id` or `continuity_event_id`, proving *why* the document exists and *when* it entered the river.

## 5. Metadata Surfaces
The system preserves context without invasive extraction.
* **Allowed Metadata:** `title`, `author_intent`, `steward_context`, `file_hash` (for integrity), `page_count`, `mime_type`.
* **Prohibited Metadata:** The system must not silently extract EXIF location data, hidden author strings, or invasive tracking metadata without explicit steward consent.

## 6. Upload Boundaries
The physical ingestion of a file is bound by strict epistemological humility:
* Upload ≠ verification.
* Document ≠ truth.
* The system accepts the file as *declared evidence* or *declared context* provided by the steward. It does not rubber-stamp the contents of a PDF as absolute truth.

## 7. Retention Philosophy
Documents follow the iPhande append-only history doctrine.
* A document cannot be "hard-deleted" to alter history.
* Removing a document from visibility triggers a soft-archive (`is_archived = True`) and emits a `document_archived` continuity event.
* The causal history of the document's existence remains intact.

## 8. Uncertainty and Verification Posture
The UI must accurately represent the document's origin.
* If uploaded: "Provided by Steward."
* If generated by iPhande from replay data: "Generated from Replay Continuity."
* Interpretation of the document must not overwrite the physical continuity of the document's existence.
