# iPhande Business Categories Doctrine

## insurance_finance
- Purpose: Financial protection, risk management, and support services.
- Typical Users: Insurance agents, funeral cover agents, loan consultants, investment advisors.
- Common Pain Points: Trust, affordability, policy confusion, follow-up.
- Supported Business Lines: funeral_cover, life_insurance, car_insurance, medical_aid, investment_advisor, loan_consultant, debt_review, credit_repair
- Recommended Post Types: Awareness, advertisement, service_intro, promotion, follow_up, testimony
- Prohibited Content Patterns: False guarantees, fear pressure, fake urgency, impersonation, spam.
