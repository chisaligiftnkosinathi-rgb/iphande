# iPhande Visibility Engine

## Purpose

The Visibility Engine is the heart of iPhande. It enables structured, ethical, and dignity-preserving visibility for local, informal, and township businesses.

---

## Structured Visibility
- Public profiles with business context
- Business descriptions and categories
- Content posts designed for sharing (WhatsApp, Facebook)
- Media uploads for authentic presence
- Area-based discoverability (not exact location)

---

## Ethical Outreach
- No spam, manipulation, or fake urgency
- All outreach is user-initiated and reviewable
- No auto-posting or forced engagement
- Content is generated for review, not direct publishing

---

## Business-Aware Content Generation
- Templates for common business lines
- Respectful, truthful, and relevant messaging
- Support for commission-based, informal, and hustler businesses
- Content adapts to local realities and dignity

---

## WhatsApp/Facebook Preparation
- Shareable links for WhatsApp and Facebook
- Content formatted for easy, dignified sharing
- No automated posting—user always in control

---

## Local Business Discoverability
- Search and browse by area, business line, or service
- No ranking, rating, or artificial authority
- Focus on discoverability, not competition

---

## Platform Boundaries
- Never expose private data without consent
- Never automate spam or manipulation
- Always prioritize user dignity and control

---

## Why This Matters
Visibility is not just marketing—it is survival for small, informal, and township businesses. iPhande's engine is designed to make visibility safe, structured, and empowering.
