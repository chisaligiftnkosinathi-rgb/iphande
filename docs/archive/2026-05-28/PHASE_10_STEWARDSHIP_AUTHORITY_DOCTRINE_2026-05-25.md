# PHASE 10 — Stewardship & Interpretive Authority Boundaries

**Date:** 2026-05-25

## The Emergence of the Steward Layer
The river now flows from replay integrity, to human dignity, toward covenant stewardship.

Having sealed data mutation boundaries, replay continuity, and human dignity, the system's core question is no longer merely *"What happened?"* but rather *"Who bears responsibility for preserving truth?"*

The greatest danger to a mature continuity system is not technical corruption, but authority corruption—memory coercion, silent narrative rewriting, and interpretive capture.

## Core Doctrine

```text
The system may preserve continuity.
Only bounded stewards may interpret continuity.
No steward may become the owner of truth itself.
```

## The Tripartite Authority Model

| Layer | Responsibility | Limitation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **System** | Preserve events and exact causality | Cannot define human meaning |
| **Steward** | Interpret carefully and guide outcomes | Cannot overwrite history |
| **Human** | Create intent and author action | Retains dignity and agency |

## The Interpretive Boundary

A steward may annotate continuity, but a steward may not silently redefine it.

* **Annotations are additive:** They append to the river; they do not dam it.
* **Replay remains immutable:** The physical truth of an event cannot be erased to save face or alter records.
* **Interpretation never replaces evidence:** A steward's opinion of a payment, a quote, or a reflection sits *alongside* the evidence, never on top of it.

## The Third Path

Historically, systems collapse into one of two extremes:

| Failure Mode | Result |
| :--- | :--- |
| **No continuity** | Chaos, manipulation, forgetting |
| **Total continuity ownership** | Surveillance, coercion, captivity |

iPhande is attempting a third path:

```text
Truthful continuity with preserved human dignity.
```

The Human Layer establishes that the replay exists to serve life—not to imprison it. The system can remember truthfully without becoming authoritarian, because the creators of intent remain greater than the records of their intent.
