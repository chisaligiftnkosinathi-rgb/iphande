# iPhande Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

## Purpose

This document outlines the standard operating procedures for using and maintaining the iPhande platform.

---

## Profile Management
- Create profiles with accurate, dignified information.
- Update location using area/label, not exact coordinates unless explicitly allowed.
- Review public profile for privacy and accuracy.

---

## Content Generation
- Use the content generator for structured, truthful posts.
- Review generated content before sharing.
- Never auto-post; user approval is required.

---

## Opportunity & Follow-up
- Log all opportunities and follow-ups for continuity.
- Use reminders to ensure no opportunity is lost.
- Add timeline events to preserve replay.

---

## Media & Campaigns
- Upload only appropriate, business-relevant media.
- Organize campaigns for structured outreach.

---

## Privacy & Security
- Never expose private locations without consent.
- Protect user data and dignity at all times.

---

## Reflection & Encouragement
- Use reflection and scripture features for operational and emotional continuity.
- Encourage discipline and hope, not pressure or manipulation.

---

## Compliance
- Follow all platform boundaries and ethical guidelines.
- Report any misuse or violations to platform maintainers.
