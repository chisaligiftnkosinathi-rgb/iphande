# IPHANDE_CONSTITUTIONAL_MEDIA_ARCHITECTURE_2026-05-26

## What is Steward Media?
Steward media is any communication, content, or campaign generated, curated, or published by a human steward within the iPhande platform. It is not generic or system-owned; it is always rooted in a governed human identity and intent.

## Why Archetypes Exist
Business archetypes encode the constitutional economic identity of each steward or organization. They are not decorative categories—they govern communication tone, workflow defaults, trust surfaces, and lawful operational templates. Archetypes ensure that all media and actions are contextually truthful and constitutionally aligned.

## Why Generation is Bounded
Content generation is always bounded by steward-declared intent and governed context. The generator is an assistant, not an authority. No content is generated without explicit human input, and all generation is attributable to a steward's declared draft and archetype.

## Why Replay Language Exists Before Replay Machinery
Replay event vocabulary is defined before any replay engine or persistence. This ensures that all future replay infrastructure is grounded in constitutional language, not ad hoc automation. The system's history will be reconstructable, transparent, and trustworthy.

## Why Continuity Must Be Append-Only
All replay and lineage records must be append-only. History is never silently rewritten or deleted. This guarantees auditability, trust, and stewardship accountability. Mutable activity history is prohibited.

## Why Humans Remain Primary Authors
The human steward is always the primary author and authority. Context hydration is read-only; no automation or AI may silently mutate drafts or generate content without explicit human action. This preserves agency, trust, and constitutional intent.

## Why Context Hydration is Read-Only
Context (e.g., StewardMediaContext) may pre-fill or inform UI, but it never auto-generates, auto-saves, or mutates drafts without human intent. All continuity is explicit and steward-driven.

## Why Hidden Automation is Prohibited
No hidden automation, silent mutation, or convenience-driven shortcuts are allowed. All actions, mutations, and generations must be visible, attributable, and governed by constitutional boundaries. This prevents drift, preserves trust, and ensures the system remains truthful as it scales.
