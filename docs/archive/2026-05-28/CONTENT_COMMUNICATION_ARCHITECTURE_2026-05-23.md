# Content Communication Architecture — 2026-05-23

## Purpose

Defines the layered communication generation architecture for deterministic, replay-safe, and ethically governed business communication.

---

## Architectural Layers

### 1. Business Category Rules
Responsible for:
- category identity
- allowed patterns
- template selection

### 2. Goal Layer
Responsible for:
- operational intent
- CTA selection
- communication posture

### 3. Template Layer
Responsible for:
- trust builders
- quote prompts
- prohibited phrases
- tone boundaries

### 4. Public Caption Composition
Responsible for:
- clean customer-facing structure
- removal of internal guidance
- replay-safe composition

### 5. Platform Formatter
Responsible for:
- platform-specific rendering
- hashtags
- spacing
- CTA placement

### 6. Communication Guardrails
Responsible for:
- prohibited phrase validation
- ethical observability
- governance signaling

### 7. Event Emission
Responsible for:
- replay continuity
- operational lineage
- deterministic reconstruction

---

## Core Boundaries

The system:
- does not fabricate urgency
- does not fabricate approval certainty
- does not fabricate scarcity
- does not manipulate emotionally
- does not conceal violations

The system:
- favors clarity
- preserves replay continuity
- exposes governance state
- maintains deterministic formatting
