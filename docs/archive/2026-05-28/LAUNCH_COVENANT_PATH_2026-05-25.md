# Launch Covenant Path

**Date:** 2026-05-25

## Purpose
This roadmap defines the final sequential phases required to bring iPhande from a governed backend architecture to a launched, human-ready business companion. The order matters. The system must be truthful, bounded, understandable, and Christ-submitted before the first human interacts with it.

---

## Phase 16 — UI/API Mutualism
**Doctrine:**
* The UI must not pretend to know what the API has not proven.
* The API must not send authority the UI cannot explain.

**UI Rules:**
* Show evidence boundaries.
* Show context gaps.
* Show provisional language.
* Show approval required.
* Never hide uncertainty.
* Never turn suggestion into truth.

---

## Phase 17 — UI Polish
**Focus Surfaces:**
* Home screen
* Steward timeline
* Media upload
* Analysis result
* Approve / reject / correct flows
* Business profile
* Onboarding

---

## Phase 18 — Business Archetype Onboarding
Businesses enter the system governed by their operational archetype (e.g., real_estate, retail, services, church/community).

Each archetype receives tailored:
* Business questions
* Content templates
* CTA patterns
* Evidence needs
* Risk boundaries
* Monetization logic

---

## Phase 19 — Monetization
**Doctrine:** Stewardship-based monetization, not extraction.

* **Free:** Basic profile + limited content assistance
* **Starter:** Media ingestion + captions
* **Growth:** Campaigns + opportunities + follow-ups
* **Steward:** Full timeline + wisdom layer + reports

---

## Phase 20 — Final Authority: Christ
**Core Seal:**
Christ is the final authority. The system is not lord. The steward is not lord. Truth, service, humility, and righteousness govern the work.

---

## Phase 21 — Policies & Acknowledgements
**Required Human Agreements:**
* Terms of use
* Privacy policy
* AI assistance disclosure
* Media upload consent
* No auto-posting acknowledgement
* Human approval acknowledgement
* Business responsibility acknowledgement
* Faith-centered values statement

---

## Phase 22 — Branding
The brand must communicate:
* Truthful business companion
* Human-centered
* Evidence-aware
* Stewardship-first
* Not manipulative AI

---

## Phase 23 — First Human Interaction Readiness
**The Sealed Launch Principle:**
Before the first human uses iPhande, the app must be truthful, bounded, understandable, and Christ-submitted.

**Pre-Flight Checklist:**
* [ ] Can a business onboard?
* [ ] Can they upload media?
* [ ] Can the system analyze with gaps?
* [ ] Can the steward approve/reject/correct?
* [ ] Can the timeline show continuity?
* [ ] Can policies be accepted?
* [ ] Can no one publish without approval?
