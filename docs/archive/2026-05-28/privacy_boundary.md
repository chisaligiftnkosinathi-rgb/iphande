# iPhande Privacy Boundary

## Privacy Doctrine

- Coordinates are private by default.
- No home address is ever required.
- Public profiles show only area/label, not exact location.
- Users control what is visible on their public profile.
- No surveillance, tracking, or forced exposure.

---

## Business Dignity Protections

- Protects vulnerable workers, home-based businesses, and informal operators.
- Prioritizes dignity, safety, and user control over exposure.
- No forced ranking, rating, or public scoring.

---

## Public Visibility Rules

- Users choose what to share publicly.
- Only business-relevant information is shown.
- No private data is exposed without explicit consent.

---

## Platform Responsibilities

- Never automate or expose private locations.
- Never require sensitive information for participation.
- Always allow users to update or remove public data.

---

## Why This Matters

- Township and informal businesses face unique privacy risks.
- iPhande is designed to protect, not exploit, its users.
- Privacy is a core architectural and ethical boundary.
