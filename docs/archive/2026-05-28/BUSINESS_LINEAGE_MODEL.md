# iPhande Business Lineage Model

## The Scalable Truth
The iPhande system does not build a separate app for every business type.
Instead, it relies on a single constitutional truth:

**Build screens once. Group businesses by lineage. Activate the right capabilities per steward.**

```text
1 app
1 replay engine
many business lineages
shared reusable screens
lineage-specific capabilities
```

## What is a Business Lineage?
**Business Lineage** is the type of real-world continuity the business produces. It defines the natural sequence of events, evidence, and economic impact that must be preserved to tell the truthful story of that business.

### Core Structure of a Lineage
Every lineage in iPhande maps downward into the system via this structure:

```text
Lineage
→ Business categories
→ Capabilities
→ Screens
→ Events
→ Evidence
→ Economic ledger impact
→ Replay timeline
```

## Established Lineages

### 1. Commission-Based Sales Lineage
*Example: Monica Twala (Funeral Policy Steward)*
* **Economic Flow:** leads → quotes → applications → proof → commission
* **Capabilities:**
  * `content_generation`
  * `lead_capture`
  * `quote_requests`
  * `payment_review`
  * `commission_ledger`
  * `stewardship`
  * `replay`
* **Core Evidence:** Quote requests, payment proofs, policy applications.
* **Truth Boundary:** Leads are not revenue. Quotes are not revenue. Applications are not commission. Commission is only recorded when there is evidence from the commission rule/source.

### 2. Retail / Stock Lineage
*Example: Spaza Shop, Local Hardware*
* **Economic Flow:** inventory → sales → supplier purchases → cash → profit
* **Capabilities:**
  * `inventory`
  * `sales`
  * `supplier_payments`
  * `trial_balance`
  * `stewardship`
  * `replay`
* **Core Evidence:** Stock receipts, physical inventory counts, sales transactions.

### 3. Service Business Lineage
*Example: Plumber, Mechanic, Beauty Therapist*
* **Economic Flow:** booking → job card → work done → invoice → payment
* **Capabilities:**
  * `bookings`
  * `job_cards`
  * `invoicing`
  * `payment_review`
  * `stewardship`
  * `replay`
* **Core Evidence:** Before/after photos, signed job cards, invoices.

### 4. Faith / Giving Lineage
*Example: Church Steward, Community Support Group*
* **Economic Flow:** pledges → giving → support → accountability
* **Capabilities:**
  * `pledge_management`
  * `giving_intake`
  * `support_distribution`
  * `accountability_ledger`
  * `replay`
* **Core Evidence:** Donation receipts, distribution photos, pledge records.

### 5. Content Creator Lineage
*Example: Influencer, Local Educator*
* **Economic Flow:** content → audience → offers → sales → replay
* **Capabilities:**
  * `content_generation`
  * `audience_engagement`
  * `offer_management`
  * `sales_tracking`
  * `replay`
* **Core Evidence:** Published posts, engagement metrics, offer conversions.
