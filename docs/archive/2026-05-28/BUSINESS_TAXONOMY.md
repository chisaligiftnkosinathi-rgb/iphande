# iPhande Business Taxonomy

## What is iPhande?

iPhande is an Opportunity Continuity System for township businesses, informal traders, freelancers, hustlers, service providers, creators, technicians, and small businesses. It helps people seek opportunity, preserve visibility, track relationships, remember conversations, follow up consistently, present their business professionally, and not lose momentum under pressure.

## Why African-first?

Most platforms use Silicon Valley or Western-centric categories. iPhande is built for the African reality: kasi economy, informal economy, township services, side hustles, survival businesses, local service providers, digital hustlers, and mobile workers. The taxonomy reflects the real structure of African business and opportunity.

## Taxonomy Structure

- **Sector** → **Business Line** → **Service Type** → **Tags**

### Example

- Sector: Home Services
- Business Line: Plumbing
- Service Type: Leak Repairs
- Tags: emergency, geyser, installation

## Boundary

This taxonomy is for visibility, continuity, discovery, and structured opportunity — not judgment. Do not create rankings, trust scores, or “best business” labels.
