# ARCHETYPE_DOCUMENT_TEMPLATE_REGISTRY_BOUNDARY_2026-05-27

## Milestone Summary
The `ArchetypeDocumentTemplateRegistry` has been physically established. This registry transforms raw document generation into **governed continuity publication**. It dictates exactly which structured long-form artifacts (PDFs/Scrolls) a specific economic archetype is permitted to produce.

## Architectural Significance
* **Eradication of the "Blank Canvas":** Stewards are not given empty text boxes to dump unstructured text. They are given structured `sections` that enforce the operational physics of their specific archetype.
* **Contextual Dignity:** A local tutor generates a `Learning Guide`; a funeral cover agent generates a `Family Cover Proposal`; a church steward generates a `Teaching Scroll`. The system respects their distinct real-world contexts.
* **Governance by Default:** Every template binds to a strict `governanceBoundary` (e.g., *"Proposal reflects declared offering. It does not guarantee outcome."*). This doctrine will be stamped onto the resulting artifact, ensuring the document cannot be misused as false authority.

## The Governing Rule
> PDF generation must remain structured: template-driven, section-aware, archetype-aware, replay-aware, and governance-aware.
