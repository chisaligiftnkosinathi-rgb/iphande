# Phase 9A: Unified Replay Reconstruction Surface

**Date:** 2026-05-25

## The Doctrine of Replay Consciousness
iPhande is no longer just an application; it is becoming a **continuity instrument**.

The next architectural phase does not involve building new features, adding monetization, or expanding external surfaces. It involves turning the system's gaze inward to observe the continuity it has preserved.

We are building **System Consciousness through truthful reconstruction.**

The system must now be able to answer:
* What actually happened?
* Why did it happen?
* What changed?
* What was learned?
* What continuity was preserved?
* What continuity fractured?

## The Causal River
Because all business modules (economics, stewardship, reflection, scripture, communication, inventory, evidence, operational decisions) now share a single constitutional memory spine (`continuity_events`), they are no longer disconnected silos.

They form one causal river:

```text
Morning reflection recorded
↓
Scripture reflection linked
↓
Content generated from that continuity
↓
Opportunity created
↓
Quote requested
↓
Evidence uploaded
↓
Sale confirmed
↓
Giving allocated
↓
Reflection recorded about the outcome
```

## Execution Strategy: Observation, Not Mutation
Phase 9A is strictly read-only.

1. **Unified Timeline Aggregation:** Design a read-only service that queries the `continuity_events` ledger for a specific `business_owner_id` and stitches the disparate event types into a chronologically and causally coherent timeline.
2. **Human-Readable Embodiment:** Map raw system events (`content_generated`, `payment_evidence_submitted`, `reflection_recorded`) into human-readable stewardship insights.
3. **Causal Graph Traversal:** Ensure the reconstruction can visually or structurally represent the `parent_event_id` lineage, proving *why* something happened, not just *when*.
4. **No False Certainty:** The interface must reflect the exact epistemic boundaries of the data (e.g., distinguishing between a claim, evidence, and verified truth).
