# iPhande System Maturity Model

This document defines the maturity levels for the iPhande platform, providing a clear framework for disciplined progression and preventing the "illusion of maturity." Each level builds on the previous, ensuring operational, governance, and ecosystem readiness.

---

## Level 0 — Concept
- Idea only
- No operational flows

## Level 1 — Functional Backend
- CRUD stable
- Documentation exists
- Replay works (user, visibility, governance)
- Visibility engine operational

## Level 2 — Mobile Operational
- Mobile screens wired to backend
- Real flows usable end-to-end

## Level 3 — Community Operational
- Multiple business categories active
- Feedback loops functioning
- User engagement and improvement cycles visible

## Level 4 — Governed Platform
- Audits active and traceable
- Corrective actions tracked and closed
- Replay continuity preserved for governance
- Quality management system operational

## Level 5 — Ecosystem Layer
- Local business discovery enabled
- Structured visibility infrastructure
- Multi-region scaling and adaptation

---

## Purpose

This model ensures:
- Disciplined, stepwise growth
- Institutional memory and learning
- Governance and quality continuity
- Protection against premature scaling or drift

---

## Usage

- Use this model to assess current platform maturity.
- Guide roadmap planning and feature prioritization.
- Prevent "illusion of maturity" by requiring evidence at each level.
- Support mission continuity and adaptive governance.
