# iPhande V1 Platform Manifesto

## 1. What is iPhande?
iPhande is a community-driven business administration and identity ecosystem designed for local workers, artisans, informal traders, and community stewards. It acts as a digital ledger and visibility engine that records business achievements and helps providers build an unalterable history of trust.

## 2. Why Does It Exist?
In local economies and informal sectors, formal business track records and credentials are often missing, making it difficult for talented tradespeople and service providers to secure opportunities, prove their capabilities, or qualify for formal support. 

iPhande exists to bridge this gap:
> **iPhande exists to help local workers, small businesses, and community stewards be found, receive work opportunities, preserve proof of completed work, and build trust through visible history.**

## 3. Core Philosophy
Our software and operational decisions are governed by four pillars:
* **Work remembered.** Every job completed, quote issued, and expense tracked adds to a verifiable timeline of business activity.
* **Trust preserved.** History cannot be simulated, fabricated, or hidden. Reputations are built on actual performance.
* **Umuntu Ngumuntu Ngabantu.** A person is a person through other persons. We grow together; a steward's visibility is powered by their community, and the community benefits from reliable, accessible local services.
* **Powered by Global IT and Business Solutions.** Engineered and sustained to provide professional-grade infrastructure for community empowerment.

---

## 4. Why Should Communities Trust It?
Trust is not claimed—it is verified. iPhande implements strict operational rules to guarantee the authenticity and stability of all platform listings:

### The Eight Principles of Platform Trust

1. **No Fake Counts**
   All public metrics, completed jobs, and opportunities are backed by real database events. The system does not pad profiles with simulated reviews or inflated transaction numbers.
   
2. **No Placeholder Claims**
   If a feature is not functional, or a claim is not verified, it is not shown. Stewards only display verified attributes.
   
3. **No Broken Buttons**
   Every interactive component must route to a live screen or execute a real, verified function.
   
4. **No Screen Without API Truth**
   Every visual card, list, and profile relies entirely on data loaded from the backend APIs. No hardcoded success simulation.
   
5. **No Payment Activation Without Admin Review**
   Steward profile activation requires a verified bank transfer proof of R120, manually approved by system creators/admins to ensure profile accountability.
   
6. **No Proof Without Timeline Record**
   A steward cannot showcase a completed job or outcome without creating an immutable entry in the Continuity Ledger timeline.
   
7. **No Opportunity Without a Clear Location/Category/Contact Path**
   Any posted job opportunity must have a real location (Province, Town/City), a defined category, and a direct contact route (WhatsApp or Phone).
   
8. **No Admin Action Without Continuity Event**
   Every administrative decision (approvals, user demotions, profile activations) emits a system event, preserving auditability.
