### Replayed Scene

Gift remembers participating in a soccer match during a school break period.

During the match, he successfully moved past several players and attempted to
score a goal but missed.

Following the missed opportunity, teasing occurred between Gift and Comfort
Thwala.

Gift remembers that words were exchanged and that the situation escalated into
a physical fight.

Many learners witnessed the incident.

Gift remembers that Nathi Neville Thwala became involved after the fight had
begun.

The school intervened and Gift's mother was called to the school.

Following the incident, Gift's mother visited the Thwala household accompanied
by Jwana Makhanya, whom Gift remembers as an older daughter from the Malaza
household and a neighbor of the Thwala family.

The purpose of the visit was to discuss the events surrounding the fight and
the concerns raised after the incident.

The adults discussed what had happened. Gift remembers that the matter was
ultimately resolved through discussion.

Although there had initially been concern regarding medical expenses, Gift
remembers that his mother did not ultimately make a payment.

After returning home, Gift remembers his mother expressing exhaustion and
frustration. He remembers her explaining that she had worked hard to provide
for him and felt overwhelmed by the difficulties she was facing.

Gift remembers her saying that perhaps he should go and live with his father.
He remembers that she contacted his father regarding this possibility.
