# Continuity Archive

## Purpose

This directory preserves the human and historical continuity that explains who
Gift Chisali is, what formed him, and why AXIONYX, iSebenza, and iPhande exist.

It is a governed human archive. It is not executable system doctrine.

## Governance Boundary

- Files here preserve truthful replay, context, uncertainty, and lineage.
- `docs/canonical/` and `docs/boundaries/` govern system behavior.
- Personal material must not be made public merely because it is preserved.
- Corrections are additive and should be recorded in `TIMELINE_INDEX.md`.
- Interpretations must not be presented as witnessed facts.
- Sensitive names and details require steward approval before inclusion.

## Public Repository Warning

The configured GitHub repository is public as of 2026-06-04. A `PROTECTED`
classification is a governance instruction, not technical access control.

Therefore:

- `PROTECTED` files must not be committed or pushed while this repository is
  public.
- Git history must be treated as durable publication.
- Explicit consent is required before changing a protected file to `OPEN`.
- A private, access-controlled preservation location must be established before
  this archive can serve as the durable canonical home for protected material.

## Chapter Method

The archive develops one chapter at a time.

Each chapter moves through these states:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `PROPOSED` | Named for future replay; no narrative is canonical |
| `ACTIVE-DRAFT` | Being replayed and evidence-tagged |
| `STEWARD-APPROVED` | Reviewed and approved by the steward |
| `SEALED` | Preserved as a canonical replay version; later corrections remain additive |

Only one chapter should normally be `ACTIVE-DRAFT`. Later chapter titles are
navigation markers, not claims that their narratives have already been settled.

## Continuity Principles

### Continuity Principle 01 - Preserve Formation Before Achievement

The archive shall prioritize recording formative events before recording
achievements.

Achievements explain what happened. Formation helps explain why it happened.
Future stewards should understand not only the visible outcomes of a life, but
also the people, sacrifices, environments, hardships, opportunities, and
lessons that shaped those outcomes.

When uncertainty exists regarding what to preserve, preference should be given
to preserving formative influences.

### Fifty-Year Reader Check

Before a chapter moves to `STEWARD-APPROVED`, ask:

> If a child, grandchild, or future steward reads this in 50 years, will they
> understand not only what happened, but why it mattered?

If not, add context without inventing motive, certainty, or meaning.

### Continuity Principle 02 - Truth Before Mythology

The archive shall not turn childhood traits, hardship, or later achievement into
a story of inevitable greatness.

Formation may help explain later choices and capacities. It does not prove
destiny. People must be preserved as people, not reduced to supporting
characters in a founder myth.

### Continuity Principle 03 - Preserve Pressure Without Bitterness

The archive shall preserve hardship, inequality, conflict, mistakes, and
suffering truthfully.

Replay shall seek understanding rather than revenge. Its purpose is not to
identify villains, but to preserve the conditions, decisions, consequences,
and lessons that shaped the river.

Future stewards should be able to understand pressure without inheriting
bitterness.
