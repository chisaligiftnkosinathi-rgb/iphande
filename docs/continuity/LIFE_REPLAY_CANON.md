## Purpose

This is the high-level river of Gift Chisali's life replay. It preserves enough
context for future generations to understand the systems without turning a
human life into a polished legend.

The posture is truthful and restrained:

> Pressure existed. A decision was made. Consequences followed. Lessons were learned.
