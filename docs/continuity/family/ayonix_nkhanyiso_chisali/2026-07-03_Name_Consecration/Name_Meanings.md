# Name Meanings: Ayonix Nkhanyiso Chisali

**Event Date:** 2026-07-03  

## Ayonix
*(Meaning derived from the foundational truth and systems of AXIONYX, representing truth, verification, and stewardship.)*

## Nkhanyiso
*(Meaning: "Light" or "Illumination" in Nguni languages, representing hope, clarity, and the presence of God leading the way.)*

## Chisali
*(Meaning: Family continuity. A connection to the generations before, the prayers of a mother, the memory of a grandfather, and a story larger than oneself. It is the living embodiment of Lineage and Continuity.)*

*"A name can be a small archive. A living piece of continuity."*
