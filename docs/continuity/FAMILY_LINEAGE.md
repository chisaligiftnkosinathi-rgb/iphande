## Maternal Lineage

Gift's mother, Ntombifuthi Florance Nkambule, was raised by Samuel Mehlwana
Nkambule.

According to family testimony, Samuel Mehlwana Nkambule became both a farmer
and a traditional healer. Family accounts state that he underwent a water
initiation process associated with becoming a healer.

Samuel Mehlwana Nkambule's wife passed away when their daughter Ntombifuthi was
approximately five years old.

Following her death, Samuel raised six children without taking another wife.

Family testimony consistently describes him as hardworking, disciplined, and
committed to providing for his children despite difficult circumstances.

## Paternal Lineage

Gift's father, Owen Sipho Chisali, was born into the Chisali family.

According to family testimony, Gift's paternal grandfather originated from
Malawi.

Family accounts describe him as belonging to a royal lineage and state that
family conflict led to his migration to South Africa.

After arriving in South Africa, he worked as a skilled artisan at Barberton New
Consort Gold Mine.

While living in the Barberton area, he met the woman who later became Gift's
paternal grandmother.

Together they raised a family that included Owen Sipho Chisali.

## The River Before Birth

Before Gift was born, two family streams already existed:

1. A farming and caregiving lineage on the maternal side.
2. A migration, mining, and artisan lineage on the paternal side.

These streams would later converge through the relationship between Owen Sipho
Chisali and Ntombifuthi Florance Nkambule.

This convergence forms the beginning of the continuity river preserved in this
archive.
