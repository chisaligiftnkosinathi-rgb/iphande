# iPhande Replay Doctrine

## What is Replay?

Replay is the architectural principle that every business interaction, opportunity, and follow-up can be reconstructed and reviewed over time. It is not about certainty, but about preserving continuity and context.

---

## Why Continuity Matters

- Small businesses lose opportunities due to lack of follow-up and memory.
- Replay ensures no opportunity, interaction, or customer journey is lost to time.
- It enables users to see what happened, when, and how progression unfolded.

---

## The Governed Causal Memory Graph

- Replay is not merely linear; it is causal.
- **Timestamps are for human context; the sequence is for operational truth.**
- The database is the absolute authority on continuity ordering (`lineage_sequence`). The frontend reconstructs; it does not sort.
- Causality is immutable: A child event's sequence must mathematically succeed its parent's sequence.

---

## Why Timelines Exist

- Timelines allow for event sequencing and historical reconstruction.
- Every opportunity, follow-up, and reflection is part of a user's operational story.
- Timelines are not just logs—they are continuity infrastructure.

---

## Why Follow-Ups Matter

- Follow-ups preserve customer relationships and prevent lost business.
- The system encourages disciplined, respectful follow-up, not spam.

---

## Opportunity Reconstruction

- Every opportunity can be replayed: who, what, when, and how.
- This supports learning, accountability, and operational improvement.

---

## History Should Not Disappear

- iPhande values evidence over impression.
- History is preserved, not erased, so users can learn, improve, and demonstrate continuity.

---

## AXIONYX Philosophy

Replay is a core differentiator of iPhande, inspired by the belief that continuity and evidence matter more than fleeting impressions.
