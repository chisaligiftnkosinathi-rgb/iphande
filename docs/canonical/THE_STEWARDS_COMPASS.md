# Canon Document 001: The Steward's Compass

## The Shift From Identity To Evidence

Most platforms ask:
*   Who are you?
*   What do you do?
*   What do you sell?

iPhande asks:
**What evidence exists that this work happened?**

This moves the ecosystem from self-description to observable reality. From claims to evidence. From marketing to trust. It transforms the platform from a directory into a continuity engine.

## The Trust Engine As A Philosophy Engine

The principles:
*   Truth Exists Independent of Us
*   Evidence Over Claims
*   Preserve What Matters
*   Trust Is The Highest Currency

These are not merely moral statements. They are engineering requirements. A trust score is derived. A continuity event is recorded. A lineage is traceable. A profile becomes more trustworthy because evidence exists. This is doctrine becoming software.

## The True Asset

Most platforms preserve profiles, posts, photos, messages, and transactions. 

iPhande preserves something much deeper:
*   Contribution
*   Work
*   Service
*   Trust
*   Reputation
*   Evidence

## The Deepest Realization

> **Work is temporary. Evidence can endure.**

This single idea explains the architecture of continuity events, trust profiles, lineage registries, stewardship, family history, and the preservation of testimony. They are all expressions of the same desire:

Let what is true survive.
Let what is valuable be remembered.
Let work leave evidence.
Let trust be derived from reality.

---

## The Steward's Doctrine

Truth is discovered, not invented.
Work should leave evidence.
Evidence enables trust.
Trust creates opportunity.
Opportunity strengthens communities.
Therefore preserve what is true,
remember what is valuable,
and steward what has been entrusted.
