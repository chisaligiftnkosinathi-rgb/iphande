# Business Life Events

**Archive class:** `PROTECTED` / `DOMAIN-OBSERVATION`  

## The Narrative Architecture
Phanda is not organized exclusively around regulators. It is organized around the **lived narrative of a business**. 

The `business-life-events` directory maps the chronological journey of a business. 
- **The actors** are the Founders.
- **The chapters** are the Life Events.
- **The supporting characters** are the regulators (CIPC, SARS, etc.) defined in the `business-knowledge` reference library.
- **The lived story** of a specific company navigating these events is preserved in `company-scrolls`.

## The Principle of the Companion
We do not ask the user: *"Which government agency do you need today?"*
We ask the user: *"What stage is your business entering?"*

By mapping reality through Life Events, Phanda becomes a trusted companion that anticipates what comes next, rather than a passive compliance database.
