# Life Event 001: Starting a Business

**Archive class:** `PROTECTED` / `DOMAIN-OBSERVATION`  
**Opened Date:** 2026-07-04  

## The Reality of Inception
Before there is a company, there is a person. A company begins because a founder decides: *"I am going to solve a problem."* 

### 1. What usually causes someone to start a business?
- **Observation of Friction:** Seeing a problem in their community or industry that needs solving.
- **Economic Necessity:** The need to generate income or escape unemployment.
- **Skill Monetization:** Turning a trade, skill, or passion into a livelihood.
- **Opportunity:** Winning a contract, tender, or identifying a market gap.

### 2. Should they register immediately?
- **The Assumption:** Many founders believe registration is step one.
- **The Reality:** The best first step is validating the idea. If no one buys the product or service, the registration creates compliance burdens without generating value. 
- **The Exception:** If an immediate contract, tender, or formal partnership requires a registered entity to proceed, registration becomes step one.

### 3. When do legal obligations begin?
- Legal and compliance obligations (such as CIPC Annual Returns and SARS tax submissions) **begin the moment the company is officially registered** on the CIPC database, regardless of whether the company has made any money or opened a bank account.

### 4. What mistakes do first-time founders make?
- Registering a company before testing if the business model works.
- Forgetting to register for tax immediately after CIPC registration.
- Mixing personal and business finances because they haven't opened a separate business bank account.
- Assuming CIPC registration means they are fully compliant for tenders (missing CSD and SARS Tax Clearance).
- Neglecting to file Annual Returns because the business is "dormant," leading to deregistration.

### 5. What happens after registration?
The moment the founder's idea crosses the boundary into legal registration, the ecosystem engages:
- **CIPC** (The legal identity exists and the Annual Return clock starts).
- **SARS** (Income Tax number is generated; tax obligations begin).
- **Banks** (A business account can now be opened to separate finances).
