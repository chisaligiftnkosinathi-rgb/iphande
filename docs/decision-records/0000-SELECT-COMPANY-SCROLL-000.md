# Decision Record: Select Company Scroll 000

**Archive class:** `PROTECTED` / `DOMAIN-OBSERVATION`  
**Date:** 2026-07-04  
**Decision Maker(s):** Gift & Architect  
**Status:** Accepted  

## 1. Context and Problem Statement
To build a system grounded in reality rather than assumptions, we established that Phanda must learn from a real company's lived experience. We needed to select a company whose journey we know best and whose documentation we can access.

## 2. The Options Considered
- Option A: Create a hypothetical company to model features. (Rejected: Violates the principle of observation-first).
- Option B: Select GLOBAL IT AND BUSINESS SOLUTION (PTY) LTD as Company Scroll 000.

## 3. The Decision
Select **GLOBAL IT AND BUSINESS SOLUTION (PTY) LTD** as Company Scroll 000.

## 4. The Reasoning (The "Why")
- Direct access to documentation.
- Ability to observe the company continuously.
- It is a real business making real decisions.
- It serves as a suitable foundation for developing Phanda through evidence rather than assumptions.
- It establishes a living feedback loop: The company teaches Phanda, Phanda helps the company, the observations improve the platform, and the improved platform helps the next company.

## 5. Consequences
- **Triggered Life Event:** Establishing the canonical baseline for Phanda's reality observation.
- **Activated Business Knowledge:** The study of CIPC, SARS, Banking, etc. through the lens of this specific company.
- **Required Evidence:** verified documentation from GLOBAL IT AND BUSINESS SOLUTION (PTY) LTD.

## 6. Verification
*Expected Outcome:*
Establish the first living business archive and validate the four-pillar architecture against reality. If features eventually emerge in Phanda with a traceable origin to this scroll, the decision was correct.
