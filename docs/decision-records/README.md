# Decision Records

**Archive class:** `PROTECTED` / `DOMAIN-OBSERVATION`  

## The Fourth Pillar
The hidden engine of every business is **decision-making**. 
Before any event happens or any document is filed, a human being must make a choice.

The four pillars of Phanda's reality architecture are now complete:
1. **Business Knowledge:** The universal truths of the ecosystem (regulators, laws).
2. **Business Life Events:** The chronological chapters every business experiences.
3. **Company Scrolls:** The verified story and evidence of a specific business.
4. **Decision Records:** The reasoning behind important choices.

## The Architecture of Memory
Most systems preserve files. A living archive preserves **memory**. 
Good records preserve *why* a decision was made, not just *what* happened. Years later, someone can look back and understand the reasoning.

**The Flow of Reality:**
`Founder -> Decision -> Life Event -> Business Knowledge -> Company Scroll`

All decisions should be documented using the `TEMPLATE.md` in this directory.
