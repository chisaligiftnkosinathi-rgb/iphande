# iPhande Architecture

## Overview

This document describes the architectural boundaries, doctrine, and technical structure of the iPhande platform.

---

# Identity & Mission

**iPhande** is a visibility, continuity, and replay-aware platform for township, informal, commission-based, and self-employed businesses.

- Structures visibility
- Preserves opportunity continuity
- Supports customer follow-up
- Organizes outreach
- Creates truthful business presence infrastructure

---

# Architectural Pillars

1. **Visibility**
2. **Continuity**
3. **Replay**
4. **Reflection**

---

# Backend

- FastAPI
- SQLite (dev), PostgreSQL-ready
- RESTful endpoints
- Pydantic validation
- SQLAlchemy ORM
- Replay-aware timelines
- Structured schemas

---

# Privacy Doctrine

- Coordinates private by default
- No home address required
- Public profiles show area, not exact location

---

# Content Doctrine

- Truthful, respectful, non-manipulative content
- No fake guarantees or pressure tactics
- User reviews and publishes content

---

# Supported Economic Realities

- Informal, township, hustler, commission, local entrepreneurship

---

# Core Domains

- Profiles
- Opportunities
- Timeline Events
- Followups
- Media
- Reflections
- Scripture Reflections
- Campaigns
- Message Templates
- Content Posts

---

# Mobile Direction

- React Native / Expo
- Core flows: profile, location, post, share, opportunity, timeline, follow-up, replay

---

# Long-Term Vision

- Local business discovery
- Township visibility network
- Replay-aware operating system
- Ethical outreach
- Community economic continuity

---

# Final Boundary

iPhande does not automate trust or manufacture credibility. It structures visibility, continuity, and truthful operational presence for people building livelihoods under pressure.
