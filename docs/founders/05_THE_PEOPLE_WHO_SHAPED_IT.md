# The People Who Shaped It

Impande is the result of many hands, many minds, and many prayers. While the initial vision may have started in one place, the realization of the platform required a community of wisdom. This document acknowledges the types of people who shaped the foundation.

## The Elders and the Family
To the parents, grandparents, and ancestors of the Chisali and Nkambule lineages: you were the first archive. You carried the history in your memories, your stories, and your sacrifices. Without your lives, there would be nothing to preserve.

## The Early Believers
To the family members and friends who participated in the first interviews, who handed over delicate photographs, and who trusted an early, unproven system with their most sacred memories: your trust was the proof of concept that Impande needed.

## The Builders and Advisors
To those who offered architectural critiques, security reviews, and philosophical challenges. To the voices that pushed back when the system threatened to become too complex or too shallow. Iron sharpens iron, and your guidance ensured the foundation was solid.

## The Silent Inspiration
To the taxi drivers, the street vendors, the teachers, the mechanics, and the quiet builders of our communities. You were the ultimate inspiration for this platform. We built this so that systems would finally see you.

## The Future Stewards
And finally, to you—the ones reading this document in the future. You are shaping Impande even now. By holding the line, maintaining the code, and fiercely protecting the truth, you ensure that the work of all those who came before you was not in vain.
