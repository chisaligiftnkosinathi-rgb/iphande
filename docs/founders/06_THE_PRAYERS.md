# The Prayers

Throughout the building of Impande, we recognized that human strength and intellect were insufficient to build something of true, lasting value. We paused consistently before major milestones to dedicate the work. 

Preserving these prayers shows future stewards the posture with which the project was undertaken—a posture of humility, surrender, and deep reliance on the Spirit.

---

### The Prayer of Calibration
*Before the first lines of architecture were solidified.*

"Lord, keep this work truthful. Let it not be driven by vanity or the desire for applause. If this platform ever strays from honoring people and preserving truth, bring it back. Spirit lead, not by might, not by power."

### The Prayer for the Hidden
*When designing the systems that would record ordinary lives.*

"Father, help us to see people as You see them. Let this system restore dignity to the unseen. May the hands that built our communities without recognition finally have a place of remembrance."

### The Dedication of Version 1.0
*Upon the freezing of the foundation.*

"Today we bring before You the work of our hands. Thank You for the wisdom, patience, discipline, and perseverance You have given throughout this journey. We dedicate the Impande Truth Engine to You. May it preserve truth with humility. May it protect the stories of families with compassion. May it outlive us, serving generations we will never meet."
