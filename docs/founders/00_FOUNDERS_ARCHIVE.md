# The Founders Archive

## Why This Archive Exists

This directory does not exist to celebrate individuals, nor to elevate the names of the original creators above the work itself. 

It exists to preserve **origins**.

Often, as systems grow and pass into the hands of new stewards, the context behind foundational decisions is lost. Future maintainers inherit the code, the constitution, and the structure, but they may not understand the quiet moments, the struggles, or the prayers that birthed them.

This archive is a living record of how Impande began. It is designed to provide future stewards with the philosophical and emotional context of the platform, so that as they extend the system, they can remain faithful to the spirit in which it was built. 

Here you will find the questions we asked before we had answers, the principles we stumbled upon, the mistakes we learned from, and the hopes we carried for the generations to come.

*May the roots always be visible to those who tend the branches.*
