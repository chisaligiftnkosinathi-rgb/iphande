# The First Question

Every great endeavor begins before a single line of code is written or a single design is sketched. It begins when a problem becomes too heavy to ignore, taking the shape of a question.

For Impande, the journey did not begin with technology. It began with memory.

It began with the quiet realization that the stories of our families, the names of our ancestors, and the sacrifices of the unseen builders around us were slowly disappearing. They were fading not because they lacked value, but because there was no truthful, enduring place to keep them.

The first question that birthed Impande was this:

> **"How do we preserve truth, memory, and human dignity so they are not lost to time?"**

From that single question, a cascade of other questions followed:
* How do we ensure that memory isn't rewritten by whoever has the most power?
* How do we separate what actually happened from how we feel about what happened?
* How do we build a system that outlives its creators?
* How do we honor the ordinary people whom history books forget?

This archive—and the entire Impande platform—is the continuing answer to those questions.
