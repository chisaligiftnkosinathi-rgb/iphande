# PHASE 16C CONTINUITY INBOX MOBILE UX DOCTRINE — 2026-05-28

## Purpose
To create a humane, emotionally safe mobile surface for continuity capture, enabling stewards to preserve reality fragments before they are forgotten.

## Emotional Posture
- Gentle, non-judgmental, and dignity-preserving.
- The steward should feel lighter after capture, not burdened.

## Capture Philosophy
- Capture must be faster than forgetting.
- Prioritize immediacy: open, speak, save within seconds.
- Privilege voice, quick text, screenshot/photo, and raw fragments.

## Continuity River Doctrine
- The inbox is a flowing memory stream, not a database or spreadsheet.
- Display unresolved fragments, preserved promises, and quiet continuity.
- Chronological replay, not rigid sorting.

## Rawness Preservation Doctrine
- Do not over-clean or force structure on captured data.
- Accept and display raw, unstructured fragments as entered.
- Honor the reality of pressured, in-the-moment human memory.

## Dignity-Preserving Language Rules
- Avoid shaming or stressful language (FAILED, OVERDUE, MISSED).
- Prefer gentle, dignified states (Needs revisit, Unresolved, Captured, Review later).
- Preserve dignity under pressure.

## Mobile Interaction Principles
- Quick Capture Input: “What do you need to remember?”
- Large, central voice note button.
- Quiet, simple screenshot/photo attach.
- Continuity stream: chronological replay of all fragments.
- No editing or deletion in initial phase—only capture, replay, preserve.

## Architectural Exclusions
- No AI extraction, auto classification, smart reminders, automated linking, or semantic analysis in this phase.
- No editing or deletion.
- No engagement mechanics or notification overload.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Steward can open, speak/type, and save a fragment in seconds.
- All captured fragments appear in the continuity stream, raw and unfiltered.
- Emotional tone is gentle and dignity-preserving.
- No friction, forced structure, or pressure amplification.

# What This Surface Must Never Become
- enterprise admin software
- pressure amplification
- shame-based productivity tooling
- forced categorization under emotional stress
- surveillance tooling
- engagement addiction mechanics
- notification overload
- rigid workflow enforcement

---

This doctrine is phase-bound and historically meaningful. It preserves the emotional and architectural boundaries for the Continuity Inbox Mobile Surface as of 2026-05-28.
