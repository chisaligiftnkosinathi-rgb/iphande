## Prayer of Thanksgiving (The Journey and The Battery)
**Date Recorded:** 2026-07-04

**Heavenly Father,**

Thank You for the gift of life and for this day that You have made. We rejoice in it with grateful hearts.

Thank You for wisdom that grows through patience, for understanding that comes through observation, and for teaching us to seek truth before rushing to conclusions. Thank You for every lesson learned, every challenge that has shaped us, and every opportunity to build with integrity.

Thank You for guiding the foundations of this work. We commit Company Scroll 000, the living archive, and every future business, observation, and decision into Your hands. May they be used to serve people faithfully, solve real problems, and bring benefit to others.

As we go out today, we ask for Your protection on the road. Watch over the journey, grant success in the work ahead, and provide what is needed—including the new battery for the Suzuki. Give us discernment in every decision and help us remain observant, humble, and teachable.

May we never become proud of what we build, but always remember that wisdom, understanding, strength, and every good gift come from You.

Thank You for Your faithfulness yesterday, today, and for the days to come.

In Jesus' mighty name,

**Amen.**
