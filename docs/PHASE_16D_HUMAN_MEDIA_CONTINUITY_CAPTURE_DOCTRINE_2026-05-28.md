# PHASE 16D: Human Media Continuity Capture Doctrine

**Date:** 2026-05-28

## Core Doctrines

- **Media is continuity evidence, not merely attachment.**
- **Raw media must remain replayable.**
- **Interpretation must never overwrite source evidence.**
- **Capture precedes understanding.**
- **The steward retains contextual authority.**

## What This Phase Must Not Become

- surveillance ingestion
- hidden OCR extraction
- silent AI interpretation
- behavioral profiling
- forced metadata tagging
- algorithmic authority over steward intent

## Emotional and UX Principles

- The media capture flow should feel like: “I can safely preserve this before it disappears.”
- It must not feel like an "upload workflow."
- Calmness and trust are paramount.

## Implementation Boundaries

- Only preserve uploaded media, generate a media ID, and associate with continuity capture.
- No backend redesign—`raw_media_id` already exists in the model.
- No editing, annotation, cropping, or OCR.
- No AI, no auto-interpretation, no forced categorization.

## Initial Supported Capture Types

- Gallery image
- Camera photo
- Screenshot upload

## Stream Rendering

- Show image thumbnail, raw timestamp, source type, and optional raw text.
- No AI summaries or extracted insights.

## Important Future Boundary

- Do not infer payment status, parse WhatsApp content, identify faces, extract phone numbers, or auto-create contacts.
- The system must first prove: “The system preserves my reality faithfully.”

## The Real Meaning of This Phase

Quietly, this is the beginning of **human continuity evidence preservation**.

Many overlooked workers already preserve continuity through screenshots, camera rolls, WhatsApp forwards, and notebook photos. iPhande is simply giving those fragmented memory practices continuity structure—without erasing their humanity.
