# The Universal Trade Cycle

Every truthful trade throughout history follows a predictable pattern:

1. **Need**: The origin of all trade. Without a need, no trade exists.
2. **Discovery**: The seeker finds someone capable.
3. **Trust Evaluation**: The buyer asks, "Can I trust this person?"
4. **Agreement**: The scope and promise (Quote, Invoice, Handshake).
5. **Execution**: The reality of the work. Not promises, but reality.
6. **Evidence**: Truth's footprint. The missing layer in most systems.
7. **Completion**: The truthful outcome (Success, Failure, Partial).
8. **Reflection**: The emergence of wisdom. What was learned?
9. **Memory**: The preservation where trust compounds.

---

# The Nine Pillars of Truthful Trade

To build a trust system rather than just another marketplace, a system must preserve nine pillars:

## Pillar 1: Identity
Who participated? (Verified Profile, Business Line, Steward)

## Pillar 2: Intent
Why did the trade start? (Opportunity, Need, Request)

## Pillar 3: Agreement
What was promised? (Quote, Budget, Scope, Timeline)

## Pillar 4: Execution
What work occurred? (Tasks, Activities, Milestones)

## Pillar 5: Evidence
Can it be proven? (Photos, Documents, Measurements, Proof)

## Pillar 6: Outcome
What happened? (Success, Failure, Partial)

## Pillar 7: Accountability
Who was responsible? Truth requires accountability.

## Pillar 8: Reflection
What can be learned? This converts experience into wisdom.

## Pillar 9: Preservation
Can future generations verify what happened? This is the deepest pillar.
