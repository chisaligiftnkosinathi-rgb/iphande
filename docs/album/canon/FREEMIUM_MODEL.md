# Freemium Model Hardening

## Free Users
The free tier is designed to encourage community participation and visibility without friction.

Free users can:
- Register
- Create a profile
- Browse and post opportunities
- View advertisements
- Receive basic leads
- Share their profile

## Paid / Locked Tools
The business layer is reserved for those who need business power. This prevents the community layer from becoming cluttered while offering deep value for stewards managing a business.

The following tools are part of the paid/locked tier:
- Quotes
- Invoices
- Receipts
- Documents
- Expenses
- Inventory
- Proof of Work
- Timeline
- Export

**UX Implementation:** When a free user attempts to access a paid tool, they will be presented with a `FeatureLockedCard` or an `UpgradeScreen` offering a "Request Upgrade" option. Note: PayFast integration is deferred; currently, upgrades are handled manually/requested.
