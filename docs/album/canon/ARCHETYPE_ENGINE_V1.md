# ARCHETYPE ENGINE V1

## Mission
Preserve deterministic business knowledge so that businesses within the same archetype can benefit from proven templates, workflows, and evidence structures.

## The Core Doctrine
> **Reality → Archetype → Template → Evidence → Memory**

Every business belongs to an archetype. The archetype carries the knowledge. The business carries the identity. In the future, iPhande will not manage 1,000 completely custom businesses from scratch; it will manage 20 archetypes inherited by 1,000 businesses.

## Archetype Structure
```txt
Archetype
├── Services
├── Quotes
├── Invoices
├── Proof of Work
├── Business Documents
├── Timeline Types
└── Metrics
```

## Reference Implementation

**Archetype:** Technology & Digital Services (`TECH_DIGITAL_ARCHETYPE_V1`)
**Reference Business:** Global IT and Business Solutions (Pty) Ltd

### Purpose
Use Global IT as the first living steward to prove the archetype before enabling inheritance by other technology businesses.
By doing this, every quote, invoice, proof, and timeline event becomes part of a reusable archetype memory. iPhande grows from one steward's business assistant into a scalable platform while preserving the wisdom already learned. 🌱➡️🌳
