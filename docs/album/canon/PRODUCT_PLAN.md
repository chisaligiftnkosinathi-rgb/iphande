# iPhande V1 Product Plan

## V1 Direction

iPhande V1 becomes:
1. **Gift’s full business/stewardship assistant**
2. **A free community visibility platform**
3. **A future paid tools platform**

## The Doctrine

> Free to enter.
> Free to be seen.
> Free to find each other.
> Paid when someone needs business power.
> Creator account proves the full system first.

## Core Value
iPhande helps local people be seen, find work, post opportunities, and preserve proof of work.

By focusing on one complete loop first (the Creator account), we prove that the system can handle the entire breadth of a steward's needs—from lead capture and planning, to execution and financials—before opening the business power to the wider community.
