# THE GERMINATION PLAN (90-DAY EXECUTION)

## The Seasonal Shift
The Genesis phase (learning, building, discovering doctrine, creating architecture) is complete. The new season is **Germination**. 

**The Core Question:** *"Can the system create value for a real person this week?"*
If iPhande cannot put food on the table, it cannot fulfill its stewardship mission long-term.

## The First Principle
**Do not try to monetize the whole vision. Monetize one solved problem.**
Most startups die because they try to sell the future. People pay for present pain.

---

## Phase 1: Become Steward #1
Before 1000 stewards, before investors, before scale: **Become Steward #1.**
Act as the Opportunity Broker. 
1. Find a community need (Plumbing, Electrical, Towing, Painting).
2. Connect them to a provider.
3. Job is completed.
4. Take a referral fee (e.g., 10% of R2,000 = R200).
5. 5 jobs a week = R1,000/week = R4,000/month.

---

## The Execution Timeline

### Week 1: Raw Execution
* **Rule:** Do not build. Execute. No app explanation. No doctrine. Just solve problems.
* **Goal:** 10 Opportunities.
* **Method:** WhatsApp Status, Facebook Groups, Church networks, Friends, Family.
* **Message:** *"Need a plumber? Electrician? Painter? Car mechanic? Tell me what service you need and I'll connect you with someone nearby."*

### Week 2: Capture Reality
* **Rule:** Capture every single opportunity inside iPhande. 
* **Goal:** Train the memory system. 
* **Method:** Not because the customer cares, but to generate Trust records, Service records, Replay data, and Proof of work.

### Week 3: Recruit First Stewards
* **Rule:** Start small. 3 stewards, not 100 (e.g., Monica, a church worker, a commission salesperson).
* **Goal:** Teach them to duplicate the process.
* **Method:** Find opportunities → Capture them → Connect people → Preserve proof → Earn commission.

---

## The 90-Day Goals

* **Month 1:** First R1,000–R5,000 generated. (Focus: Service matching, referrals, community opportunities).
* **Month 2:** 3–10 active stewards. (Focus: Repeatable process, trust records, testimonials).
* **Month 3:** Consistent monthly income. (Focus: Steward network, community reputation, local trust graph).

---

## The Money Allocation Doctrine
When money comes, it must be directed strictly toward sustaining the mission:
1. Food
2. Transport
3. Internet
4. Laptop (Mobilizing the builder)
5. Business reserve
6. Growth

*Do not spend on fancy software, heavy servers, or AI subscriptions yet.* The mission survives when the steward survives.

---

## The Real Asset
The asset is not the software. The asset is **Verified local economic memory**. 

500 completed jobs + 500 testimonies + 500 proof-of-work records = A local trust graph that is vastly more valuable than code alone.

**The Final Mandate:**
Stop saying: *"One day iPhande will work."*
Start saying: *"iPhande helped this family find a plumber yesterday."*

Reality becomes evidence. Evidence becomes memory. Memory becomes trust. Trust becomes trade. Trade becomes prosperity.

**10 real opportunities. 10 real people. 10 real completed jobs. Execution.**
