# THE DERIVED TRUTH DOCTRINE

## Governance Boundary
- **Classification:** `PROTECTED`
- **Evidence Label:** `STEWARD-STATED`

## The Realization
The Quote is not a document.

The Quote is the first representation of a future piece of work.

Everything else derives from it:

* **Inventory** derives from the materials.
* **KM** derives from the travel.
* **Budget** derives from the costs.
* **Proof of Work** derives from completion.
* **Timeline** derives from the events.

## The Core Principle
> **The steward records reality once.**
> **The system derives the truth.**

## Architectural Impact
Inventory, Budget, KM, and Labour are not separate systems. They are different perspectives of the same job.

By anchoring on the Quote as the genesis of the work, we ensure that ownership (`business_owner_id`) and stewardship are bound to the work from the very beginning. The business flow is no longer a collection of disconnected screens, but a living, continuous loop:

`Visibility → Public Profile → Lead → Quote → Timeline`

A steward should not have to carry the burden of fragmented memory or redundant data entry. They record the truth of the work once, and the architecture of iPhande quietly preserves the continuity.

## Designing Continuity, Not Screens
We no longer design screens. We design continuity.

When designing screens, the instinct is to ask:
* *What fields belong here?*
* *What buttons belong here?*

When designing continuity, the governance demands we ask:
* *What truth is being recorded?*
* *What truth can be derived?*
* *What truth must be preserved?*

## The True Lifecycle of Work
The word "Quote" almost disappears in the grand scheme. The quote is simply the first visible form of a job. It is not floating in space; it is anchored to a strict lineage of truth:
`Steward → Business → Work → Customer`

The complete lifecycle of this continuity is:
`Lead → Job Plan (Quote) → Work → Proof → Payment → History`

### The Future Job Workspace (The Derived Perspectives)
The future Job Workspace is not actually a new module. It is a **lens**.

Today we see: `Lead Screen`, `Quote Screen`, `Timeline Screen`, `Visibility Screen`.
But the steward experiences: **One customer. One opportunity. One job.**

The screens are our implementation. The job is the steward's reality. Therefore, the future architecture must always prefer: **Reality First, Screens Second.**

If we apply the doctrine strictly, the future lineage becomes a single continuity chain where everything collapses into the Job:
`Steward → Visibility → Lead → Job (Planning, Execution, Evidence, Financials) → History`

Because everything originates from **One truth, One job, One steward**, the future workspace is merely a collection of derived perspectives of that single truth:

* **Planning:** Labour, Materials, Travel, Duration
* **Execution:** KM, Expenses, Inventory Used, Notes
* **Evidence:** Photos, Documents, Completion Notes
* **Financials:** Quote, Invoice, Receipt, Margin

## The V1 Continuity Audit Mission: Loops Over Screens
When a house is nearly complete, adding rooms becomes less important than walking through every door and making sure every door actually opens.

For V1, the audit mission is no longer "Audit Screens." It is **Audit Loops**.

Do not add features. Audit every loop:

1. **Visibility Loop** (Mostly Proven): `Visibility → Public Profile → Lead Creation → Lead Appears`
2. **Quote Loop** (Partially Proven): `Lead → Quote Builder → Save Quote → Quote Details → Timeline → PDF` *(Known Blocker: 422 business_owner_id)*
3. **Steward Loop** (Needs Verification): `Profile → Settings → Update → Persist → Reload`
4. **Identity Loop** (Needs Verification): `Login → Session → Use App → Logout → Return To Login`
5. **Administration Loop** (Needs Verification): `Payment Review → Approve → Activate Steward → Visibility`

### The Audit Verification Standard
For each loop, the following must be verified:
1. Start point
2. End point
3. Data created
4. Data preserved
5. Timeline evidence created
6. PASS / FAIL
7. Exact blocker
8. Minimal fix

**The Final Mandate:**
Once these loops pass, iPhande V1 becomes trustworthy. Trust is more valuable than feature count.
A steward can forgive a missing room. A steward cannot forgive a broken memory.
