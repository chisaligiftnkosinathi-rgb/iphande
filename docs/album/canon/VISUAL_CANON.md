# Visual Canon

- Colors: sunrise gold, charcoal, township earth, warm shadows
- Textures: dust, notebooks, hands, fabric, rain
- Lighting: soft, natural, golden hour, night coding glow
- Framing: human-centered, dignity-focused, no luxury flex
- Atmosphere: grounded, cinematic, truthful

No cyberpunk clichés, generic AI art, luxury aesthetics, or over-glossy visuals. All visuals must protect the grounded identity of iPhande.
