# The Steward's Compass

## Vision and Life Principles of Gift Nkosinathi Chisali

### Version 1.0

### June 2026

---

# Preface

This document is not a declaration of achievement.

It is a declaration of direction.

The purpose of this document is to articulate the principles, convictions, and vision that guide the life of Gift Nkosinathi Chisali.

These principles were not formed in a single moment. They emerged through years of study, hardship, work, faith, reflection, observation, failure, correction, and growth.

This document serves as a compass.

Not a map.

A map changes as new terrain is discovered.

A compass remains fixed.

The principles contained herein are intended to guide decisions, relationships, business, technology, stewardship, family, and personal conduct.

---

# Core Conviction

## Truth Exists Independent of Us

I do not create truth.

I observe it.

I study it.

I test it.

I preserve it.

Truth does not depend on my opinion.

Truth remains true whether I acknowledge it or not.

My responsibility is not to invent reality.

My responsibility is to align myself with reality.

---

# First Principle

## Let Truth Be The Compass

Every decision must ultimately be measured against truth.

Not convenience.

Not popularity.

Not emotion.

Not pressure.

Truth.

When truth and comfort conflict, truth must prevail.

When truth and profit conflict, truth must prevail.

When truth and pride conflict, truth must prevail.

A life aligned with truth may not always be easy, but it will always possess integrity.

---

# Second Principle

## Evidence Over Claims

Claims are easy.

Evidence is costly.

A trustworthy person is not defined by what they say.

A trustworthy person is defined by what can be demonstrated.

In science:

Evidence validates conclusions.

In business:

Evidence validates value.

In relationships:

Evidence validates trust.

In faith:

Evidence validates fruit.

Therefore:

Work should leave evidence.

Promises should leave evidence.

Service should leave evidence.

Growth should leave evidence.

---

# Third Principle

## Preserve What Matters

Civilization advances because truth survives generations.

Knowledge preserved becomes wisdom available.

Lessons preserved become mistakes avoided.

Testimonies preserved become encouragement for others.

I believe preservation is one of humanity's most important responsibilities.

Therefore preserve:

Truth.

Knowledge.

Work.

Family history.

Wisdom.

Testimony.

Faithfulness.

---

# Fourth Principle

## Stewardship Before Ownership

A steward manages what has been entrusted.

Time is entrusted.

Knowledge is entrusted.

Resources are entrusted.

Relationships are entrusted.

Children are entrusted.

Opportunities are entrusted.

I may possess many things during my lifetime, but ultimately I own very little.

My responsibility is not merely to acquire.

My responsibility is to steward faithfully.

Success without stewardship is temporary.

Stewardship creates continuity.

---

# Fifth Principle

## Character Is Built Through Repetition

Character is not formed through intentions.

Character is formed through repeated actions.

One decision becomes a habit.

Habits become patterns.

Patterns become character.

Character becomes reputation.

Therefore:

Practice honesty repeatedly.

Practice humility repeatedly.

Practice diligence repeatedly.

Practice kindness repeatedly.

Practice faithfulness repeatedly.

The goal is not perfection.

The goal is alignment.

---

# Sixth Principle

## Work Is Sacred

Work is more than economic activity.

Work is the transformation of effort into value.

Every honest profession contributes something meaningful.

A teacher shapes minds.

A parent shapes lives.

A scientist preserves understanding.

A builder creates shelter.

A steward preserves trust.

Work deserves dignity.

Therefore all work should be approached with excellence, humility, and responsibility.

---

# Seventh Principle

## Trust Is The Highest Currency

Money has value.

Trust creates value.

Without trust:

Relationships fail.

Communities fail.

Businesses fail.

Institutions fail.

Trust is earned through:

Truthfulness.

Consistency.

Competence.

Reliability.

Evidence.

Trust compounds over time and must never be treated carelessly.

---

# Eighth Principle

## Wisdom Over Attention

Attention is temporary.

Wisdom is enduring.

Trends change.

Opinions change.

Technology changes.

Markets change.

Truth remains.

Wisdom remains valuable long after attention fades.

Therefore:

Seek understanding more than applause.

Seek wisdom more than recognition.

Seek depth more than visibility.

---

# Ninth Principle

## Family Is A Sacred Responsibility

A peaceful home is one of life's greatest blessings.

The purpose of a home is not luxury.

The purpose of a home is stewardship.

A place where:

Children feel safe.

Truth is taught.

Wisdom is shared.

Love is practiced.

Work is respected.

Faith is nurtured.

The success of a home is measured by what grows inside it.

---

# Tenth Principle

## Faithfulness Is Greater Than Success

Success is often measured by outcomes.

Faithfulness is measured by obedience to what is right.

Outcomes are not always within our control.

Faithfulness is.

A faithful person:

Keeps promises.

Acts with integrity.

Accepts correction.

Perseveres through difficulty.

Honors truth.

Faithfulness creates the foundation upon which lasting success may rest.

---

# Vision For Technology

Technology should serve people.

People should not serve technology.

The highest purpose of technology is not entertainment.

The highest purpose of technology is not attention.

The highest purpose of technology is stewardship.

Technology should:

Preserve truth.

Preserve knowledge.

Preserve evidence.

Create opportunity.

Reduce unnecessary suffering.

Increase dignity.

Strengthen communities.

Technology should amplify what is good.

---

# Vision For iPhande

iPhande is more than software.

It is an attempt to preserve trustworthy evidence of work, service, and contribution.

Many people perform meaningful work that leaves no record.

Without records:

Trust is difficult to establish.

Opportunities are difficult to access.

Contributions are easily forgotten.

The vision of iPhande is:

To help people preserve evidence of their work.

To help communities build trust.

To help opportunities flow through demonstrated contribution.

To remember what would otherwise be forgotten.

Its purpose is not merely data storage.

Its purpose is stewardship.

I will value trust above profit.
