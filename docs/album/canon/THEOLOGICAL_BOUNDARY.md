# Theological Boundary

The iPhande project is Christ-centered, humble, truthful, and grounded. The following boundaries protect its spiritual integrity:

- Humility
- No self-deification
- No prophetic performance culture
- No manipulative spirituality
- No false spiritual authority claims

Anchor: Christ-centered, humble, truthful, grounded.

All spiritual content must reflect these boundaries and avoid distortion or ego-driven narratives.
