# DOCUMENT ENGINE V1

## Purpose
To ensure that official business documents are deterministically generated by the iPhande backend, securing the app as the ultimate source of truth.

## The Doctrine
> PDFs are created by iPhande. Excel/VBA reads iPhande data.

## Infrastructure
**Backend PDF Engine:** `ReportLab` (Python)

## Required Documents
The backend is solely responsible for generating:
- Quotes
- Invoices
- Receipts
- Proof of Work
- Business Profile PDF
- Portfolio PDF

## Template Requirements
1. **Deterministic Templates:** Built to inherit from the Archetype Engine (e.g., `TECH_DIGITAL_ARCHETYPE_V1`).
2. **Media Integration:** Must dynamically pull in the company logo and relevant uploaded images (e.g., Proof of Work photos).
3. **Continuity Lineage Block:** Every generated document must include a lineage block (a visual or text marker) tracing its origin, ensuring trust (e.g., `Lead → Quote → Project → Proof`).

## Rule of Engagement
Do not attempt to generate official operational documents in VBA or Excel. The backend must enforce the layout, the truth, and the lineage.
