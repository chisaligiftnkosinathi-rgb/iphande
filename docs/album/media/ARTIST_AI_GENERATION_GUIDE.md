# IPHANDE ARTIST/AI GENERATION GUIDE

## Purpose
To guide all artists and AI systems in generating visuals that are emotionally, spiritually, and canonically aligned with the iPhande archive.

---

## Core Instructions
- Every image must feel like a preserved emotional photograph from a living archive.
- Prioritize memory, humanity, Earth, continuity, quiet dignity, and light.
- Avoid all forms of spectacle, branding, or artificial perfection.
- Use the per-track themes in the README for inspiration and grounding.

---

## Style Checklist
- Cinematic realism
- Warm, earthy color grading
- Soft contrast
- African environmental grounding
- Spiritually calm, emotionally spacious
- Minimal, archival typography only if needed

---

## What to Avoid
- Neon, cyberpunk, or sci-fi aesthetics
- Corporate or luxury branding
- Dystopian or aggressive visuals
- Overly sharp, artificial, or “AI” looks
- Loud text or logos

---

## Workflow
1. Generate multiple visual drafts per track.
2. Save all meaningful generations in `drafts/`.
3. Submit for review using the Visual Review Protocol.
4. Only promote images to `selected/` after review.
5. Preserve all generations for future stewardship.

---

## Final Principle
The visual layer is emotional stewardship, not decoration. Every image must help humanity feel remembered, not marketed to.
