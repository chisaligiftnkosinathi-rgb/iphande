# CANONICAL_VISUAL_MASTER_PROMPTS.md

# Purpose

This document defines the canonical visual generation prompts for the iPhande archive.

These prompts exist to:
- preserve emotional continuity
- reduce visual drift
- maintain philosophical alignment
- protect the spiritual posture of the archive
- preserve humanity and dignity across all visual generations

The artwork must always feel:
- remembered
- emotionally truthful
- spiritually calm
- environmentally grounded
- human-centered
- cinematic but gentle

The visuals are NOT:
- commercial branding
- spectacle generation
- AI fantasy worship
- dystopian propaganda
- futuristic domination imagery

The visuals ARE:
- preserved emotional memory
- cinematic humanity
- stewardship
- continuity
- quiet light
- truthful remembrance

# MASTER ALBUM COVER PROMPT

## IPHANDE — QUIET CONTINUITY

“A quiet light moving through generations at dawn. A human silhouette carrying a lantern at sunrise, Earth and humanity softly connected, children and elders in warm light, wind through African grasslands, notebooks and archive symbolism, peaceful continuity, cinematic realism, documentary warmth, soft earth tones, gentle atmospheric lighting, emotionally grounded human presence, spiritual calmness, memory preserved carefully through generations.

The artwork should feel remembered, not generated — like discovering a preserved emotional photograph from another generation.

Avoid:
futuristic technology worship,
cyberpunk aesthetics,
heavy typography,
luxury symbolism,
AI fantasy overload,
epic movie poster energy,
corporate branding visuals,
dystopian collapse imagery.”
