This album was created using a paid Suno subscription, granting commercial use rights for all tracks generated during the active subscription period. The intent is commercial release, but always with strong human creative authorship, continuity archive governance, and preservation of prompts and revisions.

No track may intentionally imitate the identifiable voice, likeness, or signature musical identity of another artist.

- All tracks are governed by the iPhande continuity archive.
- Prompts, stems, and revisions are preserved for every track.
- No use of free-tier Suno tracks for commercial release.
- No imitation of living artists.
- All releases must comply with current copyright law and Suno’s terms.
