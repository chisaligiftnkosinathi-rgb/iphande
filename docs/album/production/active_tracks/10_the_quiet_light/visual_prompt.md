# Visual Direction — Track 12

Visual themes:
- sunrise over open landscapes
- children walking through morning light
- elders teaching younger generations
- hands planting trees
- builders standing quietly at dawn
- soft earth textures
- wind through grasslands
- preserved notebooks and photographs
- community gathered peacefully
- warm golden skies
- quiet human presence
- nature breathing softly

Avoid:
- apocalyptic imagery
- technological domination visuals
- flashy cinematic climax aesthetics
- luxury symbolism
- ego-centered visuals
- aggressive futurism

The emotional visual language should feel:
humanity and Earth being protected gently through time
