# Suno Style Prompt — IPH-012

Peaceful cinematic African memorial closing track with luminous spiritual atmosphere. Soft piano, warm ambient choir textures, restrained African percussion, gentle wind and earth ambience, reflective spoken-word narration, and emotionally spacious pacing. The song should feel timeless, reverent, hopeful, deeply human, and spiritually grounded. Themes of stewardship, dignity, memory, humanity, future generations, truthful continuity, care for Mother Earth, and quiet faithfulness. No commercial anthem energy. No dramatic cinematic climax. No ego-centered triumph. Preserve silence, warmth, breathing room, humility, tenderness, and documentary realism. The emotional feeling should be: humanity being carried carefully forward through quiet lights across generations.
