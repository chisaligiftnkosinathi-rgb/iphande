# Visual Direction — Track 03

Visual themes:
- Tired mothers before sunrise
- Glowing phone screens in dark rooms
- Quiet kitchens
- Taxis moving at dawn
- Hands preparing food
- Scrolling feeds contrasted with real life
- Unnoticed workers
- Soft rain on windows
- Analog warmth vs digital coldness
- Hidden acts of love

Avoid:
- Anti-technology rage imagery
- Dystopian cyberpunk clichés
- Exaggerated AI apocalypse visuals

The emotional visual language should feel: human warmth surviving inside fast systems.
