# Structure — Track 10: Continuity

- Opening Atmosphere (timeless, sacred)
- Spoken Reflection
- Verse 1
- Pre-Chorus
- Chorus
- Interlude (ancestral voices)
- Verse 2
- Bridge (generational handoff)
- Final Chorus
- Outro (echoes into future)

This structure supports an expansive, reflective, and timeless emotional flow.
