# Visual Direction — Track 04

Visual themes:
* builders at sunrise
* welding sparks
* taxis before dawn
* nurses walking hospital corridors
* teachers preparing classrooms
* mothers preparing children for school
* mechanics repairing engines
* street vendors opening stands
* construction silhouettes in warm morning light
* hands working carefully

Avoid:
* “grind culture” aesthetics
* hustle glorification
* luxury success imagery
* overdramatic suffering visuals

The emotional visual language should feel: quiet faithfulness holding the world together.
