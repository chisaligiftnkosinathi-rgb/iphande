# The Story Behind: Replay

## The Origin
**When was it born?**
When making the architectural decision to use Event Sourcing for Impande, ensuring every state change is recorded as an immutable sequence of events.

**What happened that inspired it?**
I observed how easily society forgets the context of history. Often, only the final outcomes—the victories or the tragedies—are remembered, while the long, agonizing journey that led to those outcomes is erased. I realized that a true system doesn't just store the current state; it must be able to replay the journey. The song was born from the conviction that the journey itself is sacred and must be preserved.

## The Foundation
**Which scripture influenced it?**
*Deuteronomy 32:7 — "Remember the days of old; consider the generations long past. Ask your father and he will tell you, your elders, and they will explain to you."*

**Which part of the journey does it preserve?**
It preserves the "Truth" layer of the architecture. It is the poetic explanation of the Event Sourcing pattern—the refusal to overwrite history, ensuring that the past remains readable, auditable, and uncorrupted.

## The Heart
**What emotions were present?**
A melancholy awareness of how much history is already lost, mixed with a resolute determination to stop the erasure.

**Which Impande principle does it echo?**
**Immutable Ledgers (Event Sourcing).** In Impande, you cannot mutate a record; you can only append a new event. Replay is the mechanism of rebuilding the present by honoring every step of the past.

**What do you hope listeners hear?**
I hope listeners understand that their current struggles are not wasted. The process matters just as much as the outcome.

**What do you hope your children understand one day?**
That they should never carelessly rewrite their own history or the history of others. To understand where they are, they must always be willing to replay how they got there.
