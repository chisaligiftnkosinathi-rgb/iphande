# The Story Behind: The Builders (Who Were Hidden)

## The Origin
**When was it born?**
When designing the stewardship layer of Impande, thinking about the people whose daily actions hold society together but whose names are never recorded by history.

**What happened that inspired it?**
This song is a woven tapestry of two original tracks ("The People Who Were Hidden" and "The Builders"). It was inspired by observing taxi drivers, street vendors, mechanics, and teachers—the working class who build tomorrow before the city even wakes. I realized that history books and digital platforms usually only record the powerful, the loud, or the wealthy. Impande was built specifically to ensure these "hidden builders" have their dignity and existence preserved.

## The Foundation
**Which scripture influenced it?**
*Hebrews 11:38 — "The world was not worthy of them."*
And *Matthew 23:11 — "The greatest among you will be your servant."*

**Which part of the journey does it preserve?**
It preserves the shift in focus from building a software system to recognizing *who* the system serves. It is the moment the platform found its true constituents.

## The Heart
**What emotions were present?**
Deep respect, a sense of injustice regarding how society treats its most vital workers, and a protective love for ordinary people.

**Which Impande principle does it echo?**
**Transparency and Stewardship.** The belief that every action leaves a trace, and that stewardship is about protecting the marginalized and the unseen, granting them a permanent place in the record.

**What do you hope listeners hear?**
I hope listeners hear a tribute to their parents, their grandparents, and themselves. I want the listener who works a quiet, unglamorous job to feel seen and honored as an architect of civilization.

**What do you hope your children understand one day?**
That true greatness does not look like domination or fame. It looks like faithfulness in the quiet, uncelebrated spaces.
