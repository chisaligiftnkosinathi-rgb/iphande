# The Story Behind: Algorithms Don't Know My Mother

## The Origin
**When was it born?**
During the development of Impande’s "Confidence" and "Meaning" layers, when defining what constitutes valuable evidence versus mere noise.

**What happened that inspired it?**
I experienced deep frustration with modern social media platforms where human worth is reduced to engagement metrics, likes, and trending topics. I looked at the profound, silent sacrifices my mother made and realized that no algorithm could ever measure her love or her value. It sparked a critique of how digital systems distort our humanity by rewarding performance over truth.

## The Foundation
**Which scripture influenced it?**
*1 Samuel 16:7 — "The Lord does not look at the things people look at. People look at the outward appearance, but the Lord looks at the heart."*

**Which part of the journey does it preserve?**
It preserves the line in the sand—the absolute refusal to let Impande become an attention-economy product. It documents the conscious architectural decision to separate raw truth (the journal) from subjective interpretations (meaning), preventing algorithms from manipulating value.

## The Heart
**What emotions were present?**
Anger at the commodification of human life, followed by a fierce, protective clarity.

**Which Impande principle does it echo?**
**Confidence vs. Meaning.** The system evaluates evidence based on verifiable lineage, not popularity. It rejects algorithmic manipulation in favor of objective, immutable truth.

**What do you hope listeners hear?**
I hope listeners feel liberated from the pressure to perform for machines. I want them to remember that their deepest worth is entirely invisible to the systems that try to measure them.

**What do you hope your children understand one day?**
That their value is not determined by how many people applaud them or how well a system ranks them, but by the love they carry and the truth they live.
