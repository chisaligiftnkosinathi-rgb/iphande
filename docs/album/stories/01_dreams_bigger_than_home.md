# The Story Behind: Dreams Bigger Than Home

## The Origin
**When was it born?**
Early in the project, when reflecting on the human origins behind the Impande platform.

**What happened that inspired it?**
It came from memories of childhood—seeing my mother wake before the sun, seeing the pressure on ordinary families, and realizing that despite the lack of resources, there was a quiet, stubborn hope. The contrast between small, overlooked origins and the massive vision required to build something like Impande inspired this track. It is the story of the child who refuses to remain invisible.

## The Foundation
**Which scripture influenced it?**
*Zechariah 4:10 — "Do not despise these small beginnings, for the Lord rejoices to see the work begin."*

**Which part of the journey does it preserve?**
It preserves the catalyst. It answers the question of *why* I needed to build a platform that remembers people. It comes from knowing what it feels like to be statistically insignificant, yet spiritually immense.

## The Heart
**What emotions were present?**
Nostalgia, defiance, deep love for my family, and the heavy realization of what it takes to break out of systemic limitations.

**Which Impande principle does it echo?**
**Authorization & Identity.** It is the establishment of the narrator's right to speak. In Impande, authorization dictates who can write to a collection; this song is the authorization of the human soul to dream beyond its borders.

**What do you hope listeners hear?**
I hope listeners hear their own untold stories. I want them to realize that their origins do not dictate the ceiling of their vision.

**What do you hope your children understand one day?**
That the drive to build something great did not come from a desire for fame, but from a profound love for where we came from, and a refusal to let those sacrifices be forgotten.
