# Collaboration Boundary

Defines acceptable and unacceptable collaboration:

- Acceptable: Aligned with project’s spiritual, creative, and continuity values
- Unacceptable: Pressuring toward vanity, dehumanization, or exploitative identity
- Authorship: All collaborators must respect authorship and continuity protocols
- Spiritual boundaries: No collaborator may introduce manipulative or ego-driven spiritual content

This boundary protects the project’s integrity and stewardship.
