# Drift Detection Protocol

## Signs of Drift
- Excessive hype energy
- Over-commercialization
- Ego-centered lyrics
- Visual over-gloss
- AI cyberpunk clichés
- Emotional exaggeration
- Shallow motivational language

## Correction Procedures
- Pause production
- Review canon and constitution
- Convene continuity review
- Document drift and correction
- Restore original emotional and narrative intent

This protocol protects the soul and continuity of the project.
