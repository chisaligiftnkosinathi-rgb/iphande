# Stem and Master Policy

- WAV export standards: 24-bit, 48kHz for archival masters
- MP3 distribution: 320kbps for streaming
- Stem naming: IPH-TRACKNUMBER_title_stemtype.wav
- Mastering versions: Clearly versioned (v1, remaster, etc.)
- Archival masters: Store all final and intermediate masters

This policy ensures audio lineage and quality are preserved.
