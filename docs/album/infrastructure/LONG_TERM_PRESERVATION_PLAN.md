# Long-Term Preservation Plan

To ensure the iPhande album endures for future generations, follow this preservation strategy:

- Yearly archive snapshots: Create and store full project snapshots annually.
- Offline cold storage: Maintain physical backups in secure, climate-controlled locations.
- Migration procedures: Plan for future format conversions as technology evolves.
- Preservation philosophy: Prioritize human memory, dignity, and continuity over convenience.
- Metadata and lineage: Archive all metadata, version history, and creative decisions.

Remember: digital memory disappears faster than people realize. Protect the archive with care and foresight.
