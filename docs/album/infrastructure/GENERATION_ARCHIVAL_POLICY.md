# Generation Archival Policy

- All Suno generations are preserved initially; no immediate deletion.
- Each generation is reviewed before rejection.
- Emotional comparison is required for all outputs.
- Rejected outputs are archived separately.
- Strongest emotional continuity is favored over technical perfection.

Selection criteria:
- Most truthful
- Most emotionally coherent
- Most canon aligned
- Most spiritually grounded

Imperfect audio or fragile vocals may serve the project’s emotional truth better than polished perfection. Archive all creative lineage for future reference and replay continuity.
