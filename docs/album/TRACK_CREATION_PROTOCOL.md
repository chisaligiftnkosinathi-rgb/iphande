# iPhande Track Creation Protocol

This protocol governs the creation of every future iPhande album track, ensuring narrative, emotional, spiritual, and cinematic continuity.

---

## Phase 1 — Track Identity

Before lyrics begin, define:
- Track Number
- Track Name
- Core Theme
- Narrative Role
- Spiritual Tone
- Emotional Weight
- Continuity Purpose

Example:
Track: 02
Title: The People Who Were Hidden
Role: Introduces invisible labor and unseen dignity.

---

## Phase 2 — Emotional Arc

Every track must define:
- Emotional Entry
- Pressure
- Observation
- Revelation
- Hope
- Continuity Exit

This prevents emotionally shallow songs.

---

## Phase 3 — Cinematic Structure

Require for every track:
- Opening Atmosphere
- Opening Voice
- Verse 1
- Pre-Chorus
- Chorus
- Atmospheric Break
- Verse 2
- Bridge
- Final Chorus
- Outro

No skipping unless intentionally documented.

---

## Phase 4 — Visual World

Each track must define:
- Visual atmosphere
- Color palette
- Symbolic imagery
- Documentary references
- Emotional lighting

Example:
gold sunrise, dust, roads, warm shadows, night coding, township lights

---

## Phase 5 — Suno Alignment

Every track must include:
- Locked style prompt
- Forbidden drift notes
- Vocal guidance
- Pacing guidance
- Emotional restraint guidance

Important: Document what the track must NOT become.

Example:
Do not become commercial trap.
Do not become motivational hype music.
Do not over-autotune vocals.

---

## Phase 6 — Continuity Verification

Before finalizing a track, ask:

Does this preserve:
- Dignity?
- Truthful emotion?
- Continuity?
- Groundedness?
- Spiritual sincerity?
- Human visibility?

Does this introduce:
- Vanity?
- Fake struggle?
- Commercial drift?
- Ego worship?
- Artificial intensity?

---

## Future Automation Layer

Later, create tools/album/ with scripts like:
- create_track.py
- generate_track_structure.py
- verify_track_constitution.py

So future tracks automatically scaffold:
- lyrics.md
- structure.md
- emotional_arc.md
- visual_prompt.md
- continuity_notes.md
- suno_style_prompt.md

This makes the album a governed storytelling operating system—a living continuity of iPhande, not just side creativity.
