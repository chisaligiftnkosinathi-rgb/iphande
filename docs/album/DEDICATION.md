# iPhande Album Dedication

## Purpose

This work is dedicated to the glory of the Lord Jesus Christ.

The purpose of this album is not self-exaltation, vanity, or artificial greatness.

It exists to:

* preserve truthful human stories
* honor dignity
* remember unseen people
* encourage builders and dreamers
* protect continuity
* create with humility and care

## Foundation

“Not by might, nor by power, but by My Spirit.”
— Zechariah 4:6

## What This Album Refuses

* false glory
* manipulation
* exploitation
* vanity
* shallow hype
* corruption of meaning
* distortion of truth
* dehumanization

## What This Album Seeks

* truth
* wisdom
* continuity
* remembrance
* dignity
* hope
* stewardship
* truthful creativity
* service to people

## Closing

May this work remain aligned with truth, humility, wisdom, and love.

May every system built from this foundation preserve human dignity rather than consume it.

May the work remain truthful even under pressure.

In Jesus Christ’s name.
Amen.
