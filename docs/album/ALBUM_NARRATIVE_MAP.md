## THE DEEPER STRUCTURE

The album tells three stories simultaneously:
1. **A Human Story:** A child growing into purpose.
2. **A Societal Story:** Invisible people inside modern systems.
3. **A Systems Story:** How truthful continuity can preserve dignity.

You are not merely making music for entertainment—you are building a continuity archive through sound. This map ensures the album’s structure is mature enough to support that vision carefully.
