# PHASE 16: Continuity Capture Constitution (2026-05-28)

## Preamble

iPhande exists to help humans preserve continuity under pressure without replacing their humanity.

## Article I: Doctrine of Continuity Capture

The following principles bind all continuity-related surfaces and protocols:

1. **Capture first.**
   - All signals, events, and fragments—whether media, text, voice, or intent—are captured as soon as possible, before any structuring or categorization.
2. **Preserve raw signal.**
   - The original, unfiltered input is always retained. No transformation, compression, or abstraction may erase the original record.
3. **Structure later.**
   - Organization, annotation, and categorization are applied only after capture, never as a prerequisite.
4. **Never shame the steward.**
   - The system must never penalize, shame, or discourage imperfect, incomplete, or messy input. All contributions are honored as part of the steward’s journey.
5. **Never erase history.**
   - All changes, corrections, and annotations are additive. Nothing is deleted; the full lineage of every event is preserved for replay and audit.
6. **Replay before authority.**
   - Before any authoritative decision, the system must enable replay and review of the full continuity record, ensuring transparency and stewardship.

## Article II: Bound Surfaces

This constitution binds the following surfaces and flows into a single doctrine of continuity:

- **Media Ingestion**: All forms of media (images, audio, video, documents) are captured and preserved in their original state.
- **Timeline**: Every event, action, and annotation is recorded in a unified, replayable timeline.
- **Replay**: The system enables full, transparent replay of all captured signals and events.
- **Reflections**: Steward reflections and annotations are captured as first-class continuity events.
- **Opportunities**: All opportunity-related actions are recorded as part of the continuity stream.
- **Follow-ups**: Every follow-up, reminder, and check-in is preserved in the continuity record.
- **Payments**: All payment intents, actions, and evidence are captured and linked to the continuity timeline.
- **Quotes**: Quote requests, drafts, and approvals are preserved as continuity events.
- **Stewardship**: Steward actions, interventions, and guidance are recorded as part of the continuity flow.
- **Annotations**: All annotations, corrections, and context are additive and never destructive.

## Article III: Implementation Mandate

All future development, refactoring, and governance must:
- Uphold these principles in every new feature, migration, or protocol.
- Ensure that no surface or service violates the doctrine of continuity capture.
- Provide clear audit trails and replayability for every event and change.

## Article IV: Living Constitution

This document is a living constitution. Amendments may be proposed only through transparent, replayable processes, and must never violate the core doctrine: to preserve continuity without replacing humanity.
