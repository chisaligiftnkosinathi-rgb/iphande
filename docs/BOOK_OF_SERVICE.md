# Book of Service

**Archive class:** `PROTECTED` / `STEWARDSHIP-RECORD`  

*This book is a quiet record of service. It is a place to remember God's faithfulness in the ordinary moments—a reminder that whether behind a keyboard or behind a steering wheel, the calling remains the same: Serve people faithfully.*

> **Principle of the Record:** Record acts of service with gratitude, not pride. The goal is never to build a monument to ourselves. The goal is to remember where God gave us opportunities to love our neighbour in practical ways.
