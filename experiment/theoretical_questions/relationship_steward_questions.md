# Story-Driven Interview Questions for iPhande Pilots

These questions are designed to help you research, interview, and connect with real "relationship stewards" (funeral agents, church workers, stokvel leaders, etc.) to understand their daily reality and introduce iPhande in a way that resonates.

---

## Relationship & Memory
- Can you tell me about a time you lost track of a client or opportunity because you were too busy or overwhelmed?
- How do you currently remember who to follow up with, and what happens if you forget?
- What tools (notebooks, WhatsApp, reminders) do you use to keep track of your work and relationships?

## Continuity & Trust
- Have you ever lost a sale or missed an important callback because you couldn’t find the right information in time?
- How do you make sure you keep your promises to clients or community members?
- What does “trust” mean in your daily work, and how do you build or lose it?

## Emotional Reality
- What is the most stressful part of managing all your relationships and commitments?
- How do you feel when you realize you forgot to follow up with someone important?
- What would it mean for you if you never lost track of a person, promise, or opportunity again?

## Opportunity & Growth
- Can you share a story where you turned a small opportunity into something bigger, just by remembering to follow up?
- How do you keep track of all the quotes, payments, or commitments you make?
- What gets in the way of growing your business or helping more people?

## Technology & Change
- What would make you trust a new tool to help you with your work?
- What is your biggest fear about using technology for your daily hustle?
- If you could design your own “memory assistant,” what would it do for you every day?

## Reflection & Wisdom
- What is the biggest lesson you’ve learned from losing an opportunity or forgetting a commitment?
- How do you capture and remember the wisdom or advice you get from your work and community?
- If you could replay your business or community journey, what moments would you want to remember most?

## Platform Fit
- If there was a tool that helped you “never forget the people you serve,” how would that change your work?
- What would make you recommend such a tool to others in your community?
- What is the one thing you wish software could do for you that nothing else does right now?

---

_Use these questions in conversations, interviews, or community sessions to gather real stories, pain points, and dreams—so you can shape iPhande to fit real human needs and share it in a way that feels authentic and valuable._
