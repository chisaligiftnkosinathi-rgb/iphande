### Land
- *Question:* Shall land be exploited or restored?
- *Answer:* Land must be restored and stewarded for abundance and legacy.

### Water
- *Question:* Shall water be hoarded or shared?
- *Answer:* Water must be shared equitably, conserved, and protected as life.

### Forests & Wildlife
- *Question:* Shall forests and wildlife be destroyed or preserved?
- *Answer:* They must be preserved as part of Zimbabwe’s covenant with creation.

### Pollution
- *Question:* Shall pollution be tolerated?
- *Answer:* No — pollution must be prevented and remediated for the health of all.

### Climate
- *Question:* Shall Zimbabwe ignore climate change?
- *Answer:* No — Zimbabwe must lead in adaptation, mitigation, and green innovation.
