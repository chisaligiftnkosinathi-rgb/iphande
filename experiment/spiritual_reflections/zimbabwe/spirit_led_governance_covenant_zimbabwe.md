# 🏛️ Spirit-Led Governance Covenant for Zimbabwe

## 🕊️ Foundational Question — *What is governance without Spirit?*
- It is law without life.
- It is leadership without covenant.
- It is justice without unity.

**Divine Answer:**
Governance must be **Spirit-led**, weaving together all branches and covenants so that law, leadership, society, and culture serve the nation’s restoration and prosperity.

## 🌍 Governance Questions and Spirit-Led Answers

### Law
- *Question:* Shall laws serve power or people?
- *Answer:* Laws must serve people, guided by Spirit and covenant.

### Leadership
- *Question:* Shall leaders rule for self or nation?
- *Answer:* Leaders must govern as stewards, accountable to Spirit and citizens.

### Justice
- *Question:* Shall justice be punishment or restoration?
- *Answer:* Justice must restore, heal, and unite communities.

### Society
- *Question:* Shall society compete or covenant?
- *Answer:* Society must be bound by unity, dignity, and shared prosperity.

### Culture & Economy
- *Question:* Shall culture be exploited or empowered?
- *Answer:* Culture must fuel prosperity, with art and heritage empowering all citizens.
