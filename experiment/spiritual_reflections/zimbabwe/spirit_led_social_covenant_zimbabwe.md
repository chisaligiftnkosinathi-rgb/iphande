# 🤝 Spirit-Led Social Covenant for Zimbabwe

## 🕊️ Foundational Question — *What is society without Spirit?*
- It is community without covenant.
- It is family without foundation.
- It is dignity without justice.

**Divine Answer:**
Society must be **Spirit-led**, where communities, families, and social systems are governed by unity, dignity, and stewardship — ensuring every person belongs and prospers.

---

## 🏛️ Social Pillars

| **Pillar** | **Question** | **Divine Answer** | **Modern Expression** |
|------------|--------------|-------------------|------------------------|
| **Unity** | How shall communities be built? | Through shared purpose, not division. | Inclusive neighborhoods, national service, civic engagement. |
| **Dignity** | How shall people be treated? | With respect, not exploitation. | Social safety nets, anti-discrimination laws, human rights. |
| **Family** | What anchors society? | Strong families, not fractured homes. | Family support programs, parental leave, child protection. |

## 🌍 Social Questions and Spirit-Led Answers

### Community
- *Question:* Shall neighborhoods be divided by tribe or united by purpose?
- *Answer:* Communities must unite in Spirit, serving one another as family.

### Dignity
- *Question:* Shall dignity be reserved for the privileged?
- *Answer:* No — dignity is the birthright of every citizen, protected by law and custom.

### Family
- *Question:* Shall families be left unsupported?
- *Answer:* No — families must be strengthened as the foundation of society.

### Social Safety
- *Question:* Shall the vulnerable be abandoned?
- *Answer:* No — social systems must protect the poor, elderly, and marginalized.

### Heritage
- *Question:* Shall history divide or unite?
- *Answer:* Heritage must unite, teaching lessons of resilience and hope.

### Immediate Foundations (1–3 Years)

- Zimbabwe as a model of Spirit-led society.
- Citizens empowered to belong, serve, and prosper.
- Social bonds sustained by unity, dignity, and heritage.
