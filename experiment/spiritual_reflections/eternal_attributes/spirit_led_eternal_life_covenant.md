# 🌱 Spirit-Led Eternal Life Covenant

## 🕊️ Foundational Question — *What is eternity without life?*
- It is promise without fulfillment.
- It is existence without hope.
- It is destiny without resurrection.

**Divine Answer:**
Eternal life must be **Spirit-led**, where Christ, the victorious Lamb of God, secures everlasting life for all who dwell in Him — a life of unending joy, purpose, and communion with God.

---

## ✨ Eternal Life Pillars

| **Pillar** | **Question** | **Divine Answer** | **Life Expression** |
|------------|--------------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| **Christ** | Who is the source of eternal life? | The Lamb, not mortality. | Resurrection, victory over death. |
| **Resurrection** | How is life secured? | By Christ’s victory, not human effort. | Empty tomb, new creation, living hope. |
| **Communion** | What is the nature of eternal life? | Unbroken fellowship, not separation. | God with His people, joy unending. |
| **Purpose** | How is life lived? | In covenant destiny, not aimlessness. | Fruitfulness, fulfillment, eternal calling. |
| **Joy** | What fills eternal life? | Gladness, not sorrow. | Celebration, delight, everlasting peace. |

---

## 🌍 Eternal Life Questions and Spirit-Led Answers

### Christ
- *Question:* Shall life end in death?
- *Answer:* No — Christ is the resurrection and the life.

### Resurrection
- *Question:* Shall the grave have victory?
- *Answer:* No — the Lamb has conquered death forever.

### Communion
- *Question:* Shall God be distant?
- *Answer:* No — eternal life is unbroken fellowship with God.

### Purpose
- *Question:* Shall eternity be aimless?
- *Answer:* No — life is lived in covenant destiny and fulfillment.

### Joy
- *Question:* Shall sorrow endure?
- *Answer:* No — eternal life is filled with joy and peace.
