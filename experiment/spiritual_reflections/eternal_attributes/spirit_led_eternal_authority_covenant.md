# 🕊️⚖️ Spirit-Led Eternal Authority Covenant

## Foundational Question — *What is dominion without authority?*
- It is sovereignty without justice.
- It is reign without stewardship.
- It is eternity without order.

**Divine Answer:**
Authority must be **Spirit-led**, where Christ, the Eternal Lamb of God, governs all things with everlasting justice, stewardship, and power — establishing divine order and peace forever.

## 🌍 Eternal Authority Questions and Spirit-Led Answers

### Christ
- *Question:* Shall any other hold ultimate authority?
- *Answer:* No — Christ alone is King of kings and Lord of lords.

### Justice
- *Question:* Shall authority be unjust?
- *Answer:* No — Christ’s authority is perfect justice and mercy.

### Stewardship
- *Question:* Shall authority exploit or serve?
- *Answer:* Serve — authority is stewardship in covenant love.

### Power
- *Question:* Shall authority be mere force?
- *Answer:* No — true authority is revealed by Spirit and creative renewal.

### Order
- *Question:* Shall chaos reign in eternity?
- *Answer:* No — Christ’s authority establishes everlasting peace and order.
