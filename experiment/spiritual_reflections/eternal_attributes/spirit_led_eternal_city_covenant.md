# 🏙️ Spirit-Led Eternal City Covenant

## 🕊️ Foundational Question — *What is the city of God without covenant?*
- It is beauty without glory.
- It is gathering without presence.
- It is promise without home.

**Divine Answer:**
The Eternal City — New Jerusalem — must be **Spirit-led**, where God dwells with humanity, radiant in covenant glory, and all nations walk in the light of the Lamb.

---

## ✨ Eternal City Pillars

| **Pillar** | **Question** | **Divine Answer** | **City Expression** |
|------------|--------------|-------------------|-------------------------|
| **Presence** | Who dwells in the city? | God with His people. | No more separation, eternal communion. |
| **Glory** | What fills the city? | The glory of God, not darkness. | Radiant light, transparent gold, jeweled foundations. |
| **Nations** | Who enters the city? | All redeemed peoples. | Gates never shut, healing of nations. |
| **River & Tree of Life** | How is life sustained? | By covenant, not scarcity. | River of life, tree bearing fruit, leaves for healing. |
| **Worship** | How is the city celebrated? | Through eternal praise, not silence. | Unending worship, joy, and thanksgiving. |

---

## 🌍 Eternal City Questions and Spirit-Led Answers

### Presence
- *Question:* Shall God remain distant?
- *Answer:* No — God dwells with His people forever in the city.

### Glory
- *Question:* Shall darkness enter the city?
- *Answer:* No — the glory of God is its everlasting light.

### Nations
- *Question:* Shall any be excluded from the city?
- *Answer:* No — all redeemed may enter, and the gates are never shut.

### River & Tree of Life
- *Question:* Shall life be scarce?
- *Answer:* No — the river and tree of life sustain all in abundance and healing.

### Worship
- *Question:* Shall the city be silent?
- *Answer:* No — worship and joy are unending in the Eternal City.
