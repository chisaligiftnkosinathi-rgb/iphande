# 🕊️👑 Spirit-Led Eternal Dominion Covenant

## 🕊️ Foundational Question — *What is reign without dominion?*
- It is authority without sovereignty.
- It is kingdom without supremacy.
- It is eternity without victory.

**Divine Answer:**
Dominion must be **Spirit-led**, where Christ, the Eternal Lamb of God, exercises supreme authority over all powers, principalities, and creation — His dominion unchallenged, everlasting, and glorious.

| **Pillar** | **Question** | **Divine Answer** | **Dominion Expression** |

## 🌍 Eternal Dominion Questions and Spirit-Led Answers

### Sovereignty
- *Question:* Shall any other share Christ’s dominion?
- *Answer:* No — Christ alone is sovereign over all.

### Victory
- *Question:* Shall any power prevail against Him?
- *Answer:* No — all powers are subject to the Lamb’s victory.

### Supremacy
- *Question:* Shall dominion be partial?
- *Answer:* No — Christ’s dominion encompasses all creation.

### Peace
- *Question:* Shall strife persist in eternity?
- *Answer:* No — peace and harmony fill Christ’s dominion.

### Eternity
- *Question:* Shall dominion end with time?
- *Answer:* No — Christ’s dominion is everlasting.
