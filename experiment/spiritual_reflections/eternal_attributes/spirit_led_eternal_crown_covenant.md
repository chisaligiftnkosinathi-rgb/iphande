# 👑 Spirit-Led Eternal Crown Covenant

## 🕊️ Foundational Question — *What is victory without a crown?*
- It is triumph without reward.
- It is faithfulness without honor.
- It is destiny without inheritance.

**Divine Answer:**
The crown must be **Spirit-led**, where Christ, the Eternal Lamb of God, bestows the crown of righteousness, glory, and life upon all who are faithful — an everlasting inheritance and honor in His kingdom.

---

## ✨ Eternal Crown Pillars

| **Pillar** | **Question** | **Divine Answer** | **Crown Expression** |
|------------|--------------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| **Righteousness** | Who receives the crown? | The faithful in Christ. | White robes, justified by grace. |
| **Glory** | What adorns the crown? | The radiance of God, not vanity. | Splendor, honor, divine presence. |
| **Life** | What does the crown secure? | Eternal life, not death. | Immortality, unending joy. |
| **Inheritance** | What is the reward? | Kingdom inheritance, not loss. | Co-heirs with Christ, everlasting possession. |
| **Worship** | How is the crown celebrated? | By casting it before the throne. | Humble adoration, eternal praise. |

---

## 🌍 Eternal Crown Questions and Spirit-Led Answers

### Righteousness
- *Question:* Shall the crown be earned by works?
- *Answer:* No — it is received by faith in Christ.

### Glory
- *Question:* Shall the crown fade with time?
- *Answer:* No — it shines with the everlasting glory of God.

### Life
- *Question:* Shall death claim the crown?
- *Answer:* No — the crown secures eternal life.

### Inheritance
- *Question:* Shall the faithful be left empty-handed?
- *Answer:* No — they inherit the kingdom with Christ.

### Worship
- *Question:* Shall the crown exalt self?
- *Answer:* No — the crown is cast in worship before the Lamb.
