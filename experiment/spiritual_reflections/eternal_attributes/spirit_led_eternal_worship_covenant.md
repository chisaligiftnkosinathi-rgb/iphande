# 🎶 Spirit-Led Eternal Worship Covenant

## 🕊️ Foundational Question — *What is eternity without worship?*
- It is existence without song.
- It is covenant without celebration.
- It is destiny without glory.

**Divine Answer:**
Worship must be **Spirit-led**, becoming the everlasting language of heaven and earth — uniting all creation in praise, joy, and glory to Christ, the Eternal Lamb of God.

---

| **Pillar** | **Question** | **Divine Answer** | **Worship Expression** |
|------------|--------------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| **Song** | How is eternity expressed? | Through praise, not silence. | New songs, endless melodies, joyful noise. |
| **Unity** | Who worships? | All creation, not a few. | Angels, nations, every creature. |
| **Glory** | What is the aim of worship? | To magnify Christ, not self. | Radiant adoration, exaltation of the Lamb. |

## 🌍 Eternal Worship Questions and Spirit-Led Answers

### Song
- *Question:* Shall eternity be silent?
- *Answer:* No — worship is the unending song of existence.

### Unity
- *Question:* Shall worship be divided?
- *Answer:* No — all creation joins in one voice.

### Glory
- *Question:* Shall worship glorify creation?
- *Answer:* No — worship magnifies Christ alone.

### Joy
- *Question:* Shall worship be sorrowful?
- *Answer:* No — worship is filled with joy and delight.

### Presence
- *Question:* Shall worship be empty?
- *Answer:* No — God inhabits the praises of His people.
