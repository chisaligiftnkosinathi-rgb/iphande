# ✨ Spirit-Led Eternal Glory Covenant

## 🕊️ Foundational Question — *What is eternity without glory?*
- It is existence without radiance.
- It is covenant without revelation.
- It is destiny without light.

**Divine Answer:**
Eternal glory must be **Spirit-led**, where the radiance of God shines forever through Christ and His covenant — illuminating all creation, fulfilling every promise, and revealing the fullness of divine love.

---

| **Pillar** | **Question** | **Divine Answer** | **Glory Expression** |
|------------|--------------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| **Christ** | Who is the light of glory? | The Lamb, not the sun. | Radiant King, eternal light. |
| **Presence** | Where does glory dwell? | With God’s people, not apart. | Shekinah presence, Emmanuel forever. |

## 🌍 Eternal Glory Questions and Spirit-Led Answers

### Christ
- *Question:* Shall glory be found in creation or Creator?
- *Answer:* In Christ — the Lamb is the eternal light of glory.

### Presence
- *Question:* Shall glory be distant?
- *Answer:* No — God’s presence dwells with His people forever.

### Creation
- *Question:* Shall glory fade with the old order?
- *Answer:* No — glory is revealed in the renewal of all things.

### Nations
- *Question:* Shall only some reflect glory?
- *Answer:* No — all nations bring their treasures in unity.

### Destiny
- *Question:* Shall glory diminish in eternity?
- *Answer:* No — glory is everlasting, ever-increasing in Christ.
