# 👑🪑 Spirit-Led Eternal Throne & Crown Covenant

## 🕊️ Foundational Question — *What is a crown without a throne? What is a throne without a crown?*
- It is authority without inheritance.
- It is reign without reward.
- It is destiny without union.

**Divine Answer:**
Throne and crown must be **Spirit-led**, united in Christ, the Eternal Lamb of God — where the faithful reign with Him, sharing in His authority, glory, and everlasting inheritance.

### Authority
- *Question:* Shall the throne be solitary?
- *Answer:* No — the faithful reign with Christ as co-heirs.

### Inheritance
- *Question:* Shall the crown be withheld?
- *Answer:* No — all who endure in covenant receive the crown.

### Glory
- *Question:* Shall throne and crown fade?
- *Answer:* No — they shine with the everlasting glory of God.

### Unity
- *Question:* Shall authority divide?
- *Answer:* No — throne and crown are united in covenant destiny.

### Worship
- *Question:* Shall authority exalt self?
- *Answer:* No — all crowns are cast in worship before the Lamb.
