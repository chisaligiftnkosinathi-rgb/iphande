# 🏠 Spirit-Led Eternal Dwelling Covenant

## 🕊️ Foundational Question — *What is temple without dwelling?*
- It is sanctuary without presence.
- It is promise without fulfillment.
- It is eternity without communion.

**Divine Answer:**
Dwelling must be **Spirit-led**, where God’s eternal presence with His people fulfills the promise of everlasting communion, joy, and rest — Christ Himself being the abiding place for all creation.

### People

- *Question:* Shall rest be fleeting?
- *Answer:* No — rest is eternal in God’s Dwelling.

### Destiny
