# 🕊️ Spirit-Led Eternal Communion Covenant

## 🕊️ Foundational Question — *What is dwelling without communion?*
- It is presence without relationship.
- It is habitation without intimacy.
- It is eternity without love.

**Divine Answer:**
Communion must be **Spirit-led**, where God and His people share unbroken fellowship in Christ — the Eternal Lamb, fulfilling the promise of everlasting love, joy, and unity.

## 🌍 Eternal Communion Questions and Spirit-Led Answers

### Christ
- *Question:* Shall there be communion apart from Christ?
- *Answer:* No — Christ is the mediator and bond of eternal communion.

### Love
- *Question:* Shall fear remain in communion?
- *Answer:* No — perfect love casts out all fear.

### Unity
- *Question:* Shall division persist?
- *Answer:* No — unity is perfected in Christ.

### Joy
- *Question:* Shall sorrow endure?
- *Answer:* No — joy is everlasting in communion.

### Destiny
- *Question:* Shall fellowship ever end?
- *Answer:* No — destiny is eternal fellowship with God.
