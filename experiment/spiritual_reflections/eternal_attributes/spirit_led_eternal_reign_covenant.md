# 👑🌌 Spirit-Led Eternal Reign Covenant

## 🕊️ Foundational Question — *What is kingdom without reign?*
- It is throne without authority.
- It is glory without governance.
- It is destiny without fulfillment.

**Divine Answer:**
Reign must be **Spirit-led**, where Christ, the Eternal Lamb of God, governs heaven, earth, and all creation in everlasting authority, justice, and peace — fulfilling every promise and sustaining all things in covenant order.
