# 🏰 Spirit-Led Kingdom Covenant

## 🕊️ Foundational Question — *What is a kingdom without Christ?*
- It is nationhood without purpose.
- It is power without peace.
- It is destiny without covenant.

**Divine Answer:**
The kingdom must be **Spirit-led**, where nations, peoples, and rulers align under Christ’s eternal throne — living in covenant, justice, and peace as one redeemed family.

---

| **Pillar** | **Question** | **Divine Answer** | **Kingdom Expression** |
|------------|--------------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| **Kingship of Christ** | Who is the King of all? | Jesus Christ, not man. | Christ enthroned over all nations. |
| **Covenant People** | Who belongs to the kingdom? | All who receive the Lamb. | Redeemed family, unity in Spirit. |
| **Justice & Peace** | How is the kingdom governed? | By righteousness, not oppression. | Restorative justice, peacemaking, mercy. |

---

## 🌍 Kingdom Questions and Spirit-Led Answers

### Kingship of Christ
- *Question:* Shall nations serve idols or the Lamb?
- *Answer:* The Lamb — Christ alone is King of kings.

### Covenant People
- *Question:* Who is included in the kingdom?
- *Answer:* All who receive Christ, regardless of nation or tongue.

### Justice & Peace
- *Question:* Shall the kingdom rule by fear?
- *Answer:* No — the kingdom is governed by justice, mercy, and peace.

### Worship & Glory
- *Question:* Shall the kingdom be silent?
- *Answer:* No — worship and glory are the kingdom’s eternal response.

### Destiny
- *Question:* Shall the kingdom fade?
- *Answer:* No — the kingdom endures forever with Christ.
