# 🕊️ Spirit-Led Marriage Supper Covenant

## 🕊️ Foundational Question — *What is covenant without celebration?*
- It is promise without joy.
- It is union without feast.
- It is love without fulfillment.

**Divine Answer:**
The Marriage Supper must be **Spirit-led**, where the Bride and the Lamb celebrate eternal union — a feast of joy, fulfillment, and everlasting covenant in the presence of God.

---

## ✨ Marriage Supper Pillars

| **Pillar** | **Question** | **Divine Answer** | **Supper Expression** |
|------------|--------------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| **Union** | Who gathers at the feast? | The Bride and the Lamb. | Eternal celebration of covenant love. |
| **Joy** | What fills the supper? | Gladness, not sorrow. | Songs, laughter, and rejoicing. |
| **Fulfillment** | What is the meaning of the feast? | Promise realized, not deferred. | All things made new, hope fulfilled. |
| **Presence** | Who presides over the supper? | God, not absence. | Divine fellowship, radiant glory. |
| **Eternity** | How long does the celebration last? | Forever, not fleeting. | Unending joy, everlasting communion. |

---

## 🌍 Marriage Supper Questions and Spirit-Led Answers

### Union
- *Question:* Shall the Bride feast alone?
- *Answer:* No — the Lamb is present, and all are gathered in covenant.

### Joy
- *Question:* Shall sorrow remain at the table?
- *Answer:* No — joy and gladness fill the Marriage Supper.

### Fulfillment
- *Question:* Shall hope be deferred?
- *Answer:* No — the feast is the fulfillment of every promise.

### Presence
- *Question:* Shall God be distant at the celebration?
- *Answer:* No — God presides, and His glory fills the feast.

### Eternity
- *Question:* Shall the celebration end?
- *Answer:* No — the Marriage Supper is everlasting.
