# 🌎✨ Spirit-Led New Heaven and New Earth Covenant

## 🕊️ Foundational Question — *What is creation without renewal in Christ?*
- It is promise unfulfilled.
- It is beauty without eternity.
- It is destiny without restoration.

**Divine Answer:**
The new heaven and new earth must be **Spirit-led**, where the eternal reign of Christ transforms all creation — fulfilling every promise, restoring all things, and establishing everlasting peace and glory.
