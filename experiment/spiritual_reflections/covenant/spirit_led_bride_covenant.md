# 💍 Spirit-Led Bride Covenant

## 🕊️ Foundational Question — *What is the Church without covenant?*
- It is gathering without union.
- It is worship without intimacy.
- It is destiny without love.

**Divine Answer:**
The Church must be **Spirit-led**, the Bride of Christ — united eternally with the Lamb in covenant glory, radiant in love, faithfulness, and joy.

---

| **Pillar** | **Question** | **Divine Answer** | **Bride Expression** |
|------------|--------------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| **Union** | Who is the Bride joined to? | Christ, not the world. | Eternal marriage, oneness with the Lamb. |
| **Love** | What adorns the Bride? | Covenant love, not fear. | Radiant beauty, faithfulness, joy. |
| **Purity** | How is the Bride prepared? | By Spirit, not works. | White garments, sanctified hearts. |

---

## 🌍 Bride Questions and Spirit-Led Answers

### Union
- *Question:* Shall the Bride be divided in loyalty?
- *Answer:* No — the Bride is united with Christ alone.

### Love
- *Question:* Shall love fade or endure?
- *Answer:* Endure — covenant love adorns the Bride forever.

### Purity
- *Question:* Shall the Bride be stained by the world?
- *Answer:* No — the Bride is made pure by the Spirit.

### Worship
- *Question:* Shall the Bride be silent?
- *Answer:* No — the Bride sings eternal praise to the Lamb.

### Destiny
- *Question:* Shall the Bride’s joy end?
- *Answer:* No — the Bride’s destiny is everlasting union and joy with Christ.
