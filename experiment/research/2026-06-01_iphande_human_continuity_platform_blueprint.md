# iPhande Human Continuity Platform Blueprint

Date: 2026-06-01
Status: Experimental strategic blueprint (non-canonical)
Scope: Practical application of continuity method to iPhande product direction

## Foundational Shift
Legacy framing:
- What features should the app have?

Continuity framing:
- How do we help people preserve and hand forward truthful continuity?

This shift moves iPhande from feature accumulation to stewardship architecture.

## Continuity Flow for Product Design
Orientation -> Observation -> Interpretation -> Action -> Preservation -> Hand Forward

Each product decision should identify where it sits on this flow.

## Six-Question Artifact Model
Every meaningful continuity artifact should answer six questions.

1. What happened?
- Event record.
- Example: Started a business, moved town, completed apprenticeship.

2. Why did it happen?
- Intent record.
- Example: Family support, service, opportunity creation.

3. How did understanding emerge?
- Replay record.
- Example: challenges, failures, corrections, lessons.

4. How should it be carried?
- Stewardship record.
- Example: warnings, principles, responsibilities.

5. Where is the evidence?
- Observability record.
- Example: media, documents, witnesses, references.

6. How can it be replayed?
- Lineage record.
- Example: timeline, related events, linked people and places.

## Product Primitives (MVP-Compatible)
1. Continuity Event
- Base unit for "what happened" + timestamps + participants.

2. Intent Note
- Short statement of why the event mattered.

3. Replay Thread
- Ordered correction trail: assumption -> evidence -> revised understanding.

4. Steward Note
- Carry-forward guidance for future readers.

5. Evidence Bundle
- Attached files and links with provenance metadata.

6. Lineage Graph
- Connections between events, people, skills, and outcomes.

## UX Principles
1. Truth before convenience
- Require explicit observation fields before interpretation fields.

2. Replay before authority
- Show event history and corrections, not only latest summary.

3. Stewardship before ownership
- Encourage "hand forward" prompts when publishing artifacts.

4. Preservation before forgetting
- Offer continuity save points, periodic archival prompts, and replay summaries.

## Trust and Safety Boundaries
1. Consent-first continuity
- People represented in an artifact should have visibility and consent controls.

2. Sensitive memory controls
- Family, trauma, and identity artifacts require granular audience settings.

3. Anti-fabrication signals
- Distinguish observed evidence from interpretation and opinion.

4. Correctability
- Preserve correction lineage without destroying historical context.

## Practical Business Value
Continuity preservation produces economic value by reducing restart costs.

1. Skills continuity
- Apprentices inherit real path knowledge, not only outcomes.

2. Business continuity
- Founders hand forward lessons and avoid repeated mistakes.

3. Community continuity
- Local wisdom remains searchable and actionable.

4. Family continuity
- Intergenerational knowledge survives beyond memory loss.

## Suggested Pilot Surface (90-Day)
1. Family and small-business continuity pilot.
2. Event + intent + evidence + replay + stewardship note capture.
3. Timeline replay view with correction chain.
4. Exportable continuity bundle for handoff.

Success metrics:
- Artifacts completed with all six questions answered.
- Replay depth (number of corrections preserved).
- Handoff usage (artifacts viewed by next-generation stewards).
- Time-to-understanding reduction for new participants.
