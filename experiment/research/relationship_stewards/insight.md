feature assumptions

## Combined Truthful Insight

By combining the lived answers of relationship stewards with these research principles, we ensure iPhande is built on:
- Real, repeated human experience (not just imagined features)
- Emotional and operational pain points that recur
- Natural workflows and memory systems that already work (even if fragile)
- A deep respect for what must remain human, and what can be safely digitized

This approach lets us:
- Identify the first archetypes to serve
- Map the most urgent continuity gaps
- Design replay and memory features that fit real life
- Avoid disrupting what is sacred or effective in the current human system

Truthful systems are born at the intersection of observed reality and careful, respectful design.
