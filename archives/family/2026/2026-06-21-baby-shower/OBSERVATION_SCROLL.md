# Observation Scroll

Date: 2026-06-21

## The Celebration of a Promise

A child was celebrated before entering the world.

Family and friends gathered around a promise that was still growing.

Observation:

The value of life is recognized before achievement,
before titles,
before accomplishments.

The child was celebrated simply because life was present.

Just as a seed is honored before it becomes a tree,
a child is celebrated before birth.

## Themes

* Legacy
* Family
* Promise
* Expectation
* Remembrance
* Stewardship

## Reflection

Many important things in life are first received by faith,
then witnessed by sight.
